The Ready-Made Gang

No time to make cats? Meet the Bin Lid Gang. Pick one each and start robbing. With three players, leave out Pip and one other; with four, leave out Pip.

Two-Bin Tilly

“Every lid on this street has a trick. I know all of them.”

A grey tabby with green eyes and a red collar she got from nobody knows where, with a brass tag that says nothing at all. She’s the Brains: she knows how doors open, which bins are full on Tuesdays, and the way over every fence.

Big Biscuit

“Excuse me. I’m coming through. Thank you.”

A big brown tabby with a cream muzzle, amber eyes and a notch in his left ear that he got from a fox, or a fence, depending on who’s asking. He’s the Muscle. Dogs think twice about him. So does the gang, when there’s only one sardine left.

Velvet

“Hello. Yes. It’s me. I know.”

A sleek black cat with amber eyes, a white star on her chest and one white sock on her left forepaw. She’s the Charmer: one slow blink and a grown-up forgets what they came into the room for. She was somebody’s pet once. She doesn’t talk about it.

Pip

“Is that a string? Is that a STRING?”

The gang’s kitten: a fluffy white calico with a ginger patch over her left eye, a black right ear, black spots and a black tail tip, and big blue eyes. Humans melt. Then she knocks their tea over. She’s small enough to squeeze through anything.
