Chapter 3

Meet the Gang

No time to make cats? Meet the Nine Lives Gang: five alley cats who think they own this street. Pick one each and get to work. With three players, leave out Pip and one of Tilly, Biscuit and Mags — keep Velvet, because every gang needs a charmer; with four, leave out Pip.

Two-Bin Tilly

“Every lid on this street has a trick. I invented most of them.”

A grey tabby with green eyes, a brass stud in her ear, a fishbone tattoo on her right foreleg, and a red collar with a brass tag that says nothing at all. She’s the Brains: she knows how doors open, which bins are full on Tuesdays, and the way over every fence.

Big Biscuit

“I’m not saying move. I’m just saying I’m coming through.”

A big brown tabby with a cream muzzle, amber eyes, a notch in his left ear, a black bandana on his head, a chunky gold chain, and the gang’s sign — a cat’s head with a 9 — tattooed on his right shoulder. He’s the Muscle. Dogs think twice about him. So does the gang, when there’s only one sardine left.

Velvet

“I don’t break in. I get invited.”

A sleek black cat with amber eyes, a white star on her chest and a white sock on her left forepaw. No bandana, no tattoo: the Charmer has to pass for a pet. One slow blink and a grown-up forgets what they came in for. She was a pet once. She doesn’t talk about it.

Marmalade Mags

“You didn’t see me. Nobody ever does.”

A ginger tabby with green eyes, a small gold hoop in her left ear, a navy bandana with white dots round her neck and a kink at the tip of her tail. She’s the Shadow: you won’t see her come in or leave, and the chicken will be gone. If there’s a box in the house, she’s in it.

Pip

“I’m the toughest. Is that a STRING?”

The gang’s kitten: a fluffy white calico with a ginger patch over her left eye, a black right ear, black spots and tail tip, and big blue eyes. Her pale blue bandana is far too big, and her medal is a shiny bottle cap. Humans melt. Then she knocks their tea over. She fits through any Tight gap.
