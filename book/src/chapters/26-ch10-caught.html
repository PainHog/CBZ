Chapter 10

Caught!

“Who’s a good kitty? Who’s a lovely good kitty? Are you hungry? You can stay here with me.” — the end of many a street cat’s career

Getting chased out with a broom is undignified, but you can always come back in another way. Getting picked up is worse. Humans who pick up cats tend to keep them.

Held

When Cuddle fills, a human picks the cat up. A Held cat can’t move, and drops anything it was carrying. While the human is holding a cat they stay where they are: they don’t follow their routine, answer noise or Search, and they don’t find things. But they still see everything in their room.

On its turn a held cat can wriggle free (Savage, Tricky; Chonky cats get +1 die; cats in the same room can help). If it works, the cat drops to the floor in that room, seen. Or it can go limp and purr, and let the gang get on with it.

In the house’s turn (after the routines), the human holding a cat rolls a d6:

d6What the human does
1–3Keeps cuddling.
4–5Carries the cat to the Den — the house’s spare room or bathroom — and shuts it in, “just until we find your owner”. The human stays just outside, in the room next to the Den’s door, for the rest of this round.
6Puts the cat out through the nearest entrance that works this round (it stays open): “Go on, home you go.”

Shut in

A cat in the Den is Shut In; its door stays shut while a cat is inside. A friend in the next room can open it (Streetwise, Tricky — jump for the handle). The cat on its own needs Streetwise, Hard. Once the door is open, the cat is free.

Pinched

Any cat still inside when the robbery in the house ends — held, shut in or free — is pinched. The humans have rung animal control, and the warden carries it out to the van. So is a cat the warden catches in the getaway (Chapter 9). A pinched cat goes to the pound: a cage, a bowl, a blanket, and a lot of time to think about what it did.

In a one-off robbery, while a cat is held or shut in, its player can play a resident if the Storyteller likes — the house cat, perhaps, who is delighted to see it go. In a campaign, read on.

Don’t all run while a friend is held or shut in. The robbery in the house ends at the end of any round when no free cat is inside and the gang has run for it (Chapter 8) — and then the friend still inside is pinched.

Nine Lives

Every cat has 9 Lives. They only matter in a campaign (Chapter 14). When a cat is pinched, its player can spend a Life: the cat busts out of the pound in the night and is back with the gang by the next robbery. With no Lives left, somebody adopts it from the pound, and it lives on a lap for ever.

Nobody gets hurt

Describe every catch as cartoon slapstick: the broom sweeps, the cat skids out of the door and lands on its feet; the scoop is all “ooh, look at you”. The worst thing that can happen is the pound — and the very worst is love.
