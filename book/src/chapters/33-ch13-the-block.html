Chapter 13

The Block

One robbery makes a great evening. A street full of houses makes a campaign: the gang gets a name, a reputation, and a list of humans who now lock their windows.

Playing a campaign

Each session is one robbery at one house on the Block. Draw a simple map of the street, and add houses as the gang hears about them: the five in Part Four, and any you make.

What carries over

ThingBetween robberies
CattitudeBack to 2 each robbery.
LivesCarry over. Spend one when a cat is adopted to have it escape before the next robbery.
RepThe gang gets 1 Rep for every Clean Getaway.
HousesA house the gang has robbed before starts with Patience 1 lower (never below 2). They remember.

Rep

At 3, 6 and 9 Rep, every cat in the gang raises one approach by 1 (never above 4). A cat who joins later starts with as many raises as the gang has had.

Adopted cats

A cat adopted with no Lives to spend leaves the gang, and its player makes a new cat. The old one isn’t gone: it lives somewhere on the Block now. One day the gang may rob that very house — and meet a familiar face by the radiator, who might help them, or might not.

A cat’s last Life

When a cat spends its ninth Life, it has nothing left to spend: the next time it’s adopted, it stays. Most players find their cat a very happy ending there.
