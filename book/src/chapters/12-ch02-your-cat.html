Chapter 2

Making Your Cat

Every cat in the gang is a stray with a street name, a coat, a bit of bling and a way of doing things. Making one takes about five minutes. Pick from each table, or roll for it — or roll and keep what makes you laugh.

Seven steps

  1. A street name.
  2. A coat, a mark, and eyes.
  3. Your approaches: 3, 2, 1, 1.
  4. Two instincts.
  5. A body: Lean or Chonky.
  6. A quirk.
  7. Cattitude 2 and 9 Lives.

1. A street name

Roll a d6 on each column, or pick. If another cat in the gang already has that name, roll the Name column again, or pick a name nobody has. Two-Bin Tilly got her name from the two bins she sleeps between.

No d8? Roll a d6 until it shows 1–4. Then roll it once more: on a 1–3 keep the first number, on a 4–6 add 4 to it. That gives 1 to 8, all equally likely.

3. Your approaches

Every cat does things in four ways. Give your cat the ratings 3, 2, 1 and 1, one to each approach, in any order. A rating is how many dice you roll when you do something that way.

ApproachWhen you use itThe gang calls you…
SneakyDoing things without being noticed: hiding, slipping past, creeping, snatching something from under a nose.the Shadow
SweetCharming humans and soft pets: rubbing round legs, purring, the slow blink, begging, being let in and out.the Charmer
SavageForce and speed: hissing, swatting, shoving, bolting, facing down a dog, wriggling free.the Muscle
StreetwiseKnowing how things work: door handles, latches, cupboards, bags, the shortcut over the shed.the Brains

Your 3 is your role. A good crew has a bit of everything.

To roll instead, roll a d6 for the approach that gets the 3: 1 Sneaky, 2 Sweet, 3 Savage, 4 Streetwise, 5–6 roll again. In a gang of three, four or five, if another cat already has its 3 there, move along that list to the next approach nobody has at 3 yet (after Streetwise, go round to Sneaky), so no two cats share a 3; a fifth cat keeps what it rolled. Then roll a d6 for the 2 and count along the list from your 3: 1–2 the next approach, 3–4 the one after, 5–6 the one after that. The other two get 1.

4. Two instincts

Roll a d8 twice, rerolling if you get the same one twice, or pick two. Each instinct gives you a Knack — an extra die when what you’re doing suits it — and a temptation, something you find very hard to walk past. Chapter 7 says exactly what each one does.

No d8? Use the same trick as for coats: roll a d6 until it shows 1–4, then roll once more and add 4 on a 4–6.

5. A body

Roll a d6 or pick: 1–3 Lean, 4–6 Chonky.

  • Lean cats fit through Tight gaps: a cracked window, a gap under a gate. They can carry Small things.
  • Chonky cats can’t fit through Tight gaps, but they can carry a Medium thing on their own, and they get +1 die to wriggle free when a human picks them up. There’s a lot of them to hold.

6. A quirk

Roll a d6 or make one up. Quirks are for fun; they never change the rules.

d6Quirk
1Hates the rain more than anything in the world.
2Collects bottle caps under the bakery steps.
3Was somebody’s pet, once. Doesn’t talk about it.
4Never forgets a face, especially a face with a broom.
5Sleeps on the warm roof of the launderette.
6Talks to pigeons. The pigeons don’t talk back.

7. Cattitude and Lives

Every cat starts each robbery with 2 Cattitude (take two tokens; 3 in a gang of three). You can hold up to 5. You earn it by giving in to your instincts and by rolling Purrfects, and you spend it to Push a roll or to resist a temptation (Chapter 4). Spend it — it doesn’t carry over to the next robbery.

Every cat has 9 Lives. They only matter in a campaign (Chapter 14): when a cat gets pinched, a Life busts it out of the pound and back to the gang.

The gang

Give your gang a name. Roll a d6 or pick: 1 the Bottle Cap Bandits, 2 the Lamppost Mob, 3 the Dustbin Dozen (however many you are), 4 the Midnight Yowlers, 5 the Sticky Paw Syndicate, 6 the Back Fence Crew.

Example: making Marmalade Mags

Lena rolls a 4 and a 4: Lucky Mags. She picks “Marmalade Mags” instead: it sounds tougher. She rolls a ginger tabby coat, a kinked tail and green eyes. She puts her 3 in Sneaky and her 2 in Savage. She rolls a 3 and a 4 on the d8: Box Sitter and Hunter. She rolls a 2 for her body: Lean. Her quirk is a 1: she hates the rain. She takes 2 Cattitude tokens and writes 9 Lives.
