Counting dice (Ch 4)

Approach + Knack (+1) + helpers (+1 each, max 2), never more than 5. Then take away: Tricky −1, Hard −2, a pet’s rating, and on Sneaky or Sweet the watcher’s Attention (the highest). 0 or fewer: roll 2, keep the lower, no better than a Scrape.

Push (Ch 4)

Once a roll: reroll every die that isn’t a 6. Pay 1 Cattitude, or 1 step on that roll’s meter (it always lands, even on a Purrfect).

Shoo! (Broom full, Ch 5)

Out through the nearest entrance the cat fits that works this round (opened if shut), then shut — unless it’s the last one open. If it carried the prize: the prize goes back to its Clock spot, steps undone. Meter to 0, Patience −1 (min 2); the other meter drops to one below Patience if it’s there.

Scooped! (Cuddle full, Ch 5, 10)

Held, as above. The holder still sees the room. Holder’s d6 in the house’s turn: 1–3 cuddles · 4–5 the Den · 6 outside. Wriggle free: Savage, Tricky (Chonky +1; friends help). Den door: a friend next door Streetwise, Tricky; alone, Hard.

Temptation (Ch 7)

One cat a round, never the same cat twice running. Give in: +1 Cattitude; the instinct thing is your Action, first, before you move; never fills a meter. Resist: −1 Cattitude (none? give in).

Noise, lures and Distract (Ch 6)

Each noise draws the closest awake human within 2 rooms who isn’t there already. A noise made on purpose leaves a mess (+1 Broom when found). Distract (Sweet): that human sees nobody until the house’s turn; each earlier distraction of them costs a die.

Pets (Ch 11)

In an awake dog’s room, deal with it first. Savage drives it off for 3 rounds · Sweet befriends a soft one · Sneaky slips past this turn · Flop: chased back. A parrot: cover the cage (Streetwise, Tricky).

In and out (Ch 8)

Front door: its rounds only, one cat a round (Sneaky, Hard, or Sweet). Reopen a shut entrance from inside: Streetwise, Hard. Meow at a door where a human is: Sweet (not with your mouth full).

The prize (Ch 8)

Hauling a Medium or Large prize, and eating a portion, are always rolls (Sneaky or Savage; plain unless the house says). Carriers can’t hide. Finding it, end of the house’s turn: a Searching human sees a cat +1 Broom · anyone not yet Searching beside the disturbed prize +1 Broom and Searching · a mess found +1 Broom.

The getaway (Ch 9)

When the house is done, anyone still inside is pinched; everyone else runs four obstacles to the hideout with the loot (food: its portions; else Small 1, Medium 2, Large 3 pieces; one each, Chonky two; carrying = −1 die, drop a piece to lose it). A Flop: stuck, carriers drop a piece. The warden arrives after round 1 (round 2 if nobody was caught tonight), then moves 1 space a round, 2 after a Flop. On or behind the warden: dodge (Sneaky, Savage or Sweet, plain) or be pinched.

How it went (Ch 9)

Loot = pieces home + portions eaten inside. Clean Getaway: all of it, nobody pinched · Messy: at least half, or all but someone pinched · Busted: less. Lights Out, two rounds after the prize is gone: any cat inside is pinched. Don’t run while a friend is held.
