You saw it go in. A chicken. A cake. A box.

You’re a gang of street cats, and there is something in that house you want very, very badly. Slip in through the cat flap, charm the grandad, scare off the dog — but don’t get caught. Humans don’t mind cats. They mind cats who do things. Push it too far and out comes the broom. Be too sweet and somebody picks you up and keeps you for ever.

For 3 to 5 players and a Storyteller, ages 8 and up, one to two hours a robbery. Five houses ready to play, a ready-made gang, and rules to build your own street.

Being seen isn’t being caught. Being seen doing something is.
