You saw it go in. A chicken. A box. A ring.

You’re a gang of alley-cat toughs — big talk, bad ideas, bandanas and very sticky paws — and there’s something in that house you want. So you’re going in. Barge through the cat flap, sweet-talk the grandad, stare down the dog, grab the loot. Humans don’t mind cats. They mind cats who do things. Push it too far and out comes the broom. Be too sweet and somebody scoops you up and won’t let go. Then it’s over the fences with the loot, dropping bits as you go, while the Five-O — the animal control warden — puffs along behind you with a net.

For 3 to 5 players and a Storyteller, ages 8 and up, one to two hours a job. Five houses ready to hit, a ready-made gang, and rules to build your own street.

Nine lives. One getaway. Make it count.
