Chapter 10

Who Lives There

Every house has its people and its pets, and every one of them has a routine. The Storyteller plays them all — fairly, by the dice.

Humans

Each human has an Attention of 0, 1 or 2: how hard they are to fool. When a human is watching, their Attention takes dice away from Sneaky and Sweet rolls (only the highest Attention counts if several are watching). Savage rolls ignore it — but Savage never goes unnoticed.

Each human has a d6 routine: six results, each “stays put” or a room. At the end of every round the Storyteller rolls for each human who didn’t answer a noise, and they go straight to that room. They only see the room they end up in.

Searching

Searching

A human who walks in and finds the prize disturbed — or goes to fetch it and finds it gone — adds +1 Broom and starts Searching for the rest of the robbery. At the end of each round, instead of following their routine, a Searching human:

  1. stays put if they can see a cat in their room; otherwise
  2. steps into a next room where they can see a cat (a room with cats carrying the prize first; the Storyteller picks between others); otherwise
  3. follows their routine as usual.

Hidden cats aren’t seen, so a Searching human walks right past them.

Pets

Pets have a rating of 1 to 3 (how much dice they take away when they’re in your way) and a d6 routine like a human’s. A routine result of sniff means the pet goes one room towards the nearest cat. Pets don’t fill the Mood; they make noise, and they get in the way. A pet that is driven off hides somewhere for 3 rounds, then comes back to its usual room.

Asleep
Some dogs start asleep. A sleeping dog does nothing until there’s noise in its room or the next one; then it wakes and follows its routine.
Bark
An awake dog barks — noise — at the end of any round a cat is in its room. Dogs smell hidden cats.
Charge
A cat that walks into an awake dog’s room, or starts its turn there, must leave or deal with the dog before doing anything else.
Beat it
Savage drives it off (it hides for 3 rounds). Sweet befriends a soft dog for the rest of the robbery. Sneaky slips past it for this turn. A Flop: the dog chases you into the next room, barking.
