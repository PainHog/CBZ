Chapter 5

Seen, and the Mood

“Oh, hello! Whose cat are you? … Is that my chicken?”

A spider in your kitchen is a problem. A cat in your kitchen is just a cat. Humans don’t mind cats wandering about — until a cat does something. That is the heart of this game: being seen isn’t being caught. Being seen doing something is.

Who sees you

You are seen when you are in the same room as a human who is awake and isn’t Distracted — and you aren’t in a hiding spot. (A human holding a cat still sees you, but their hands are full: they can’t chase or pick up anyone else until they let go.) Humans never see into the next room. Pets see too, but they don’t fill the Mood: the dog has its own ways of making trouble (Chapter 10).

Hiding spots. Every room has somewhere to hide — under the sofa, behind the curtain, on top of the wardrobe — unless the house says it hasn’t. Getting into one is a simple Action if nobody sees you, or a Sneaky roll if somebody does. You stay hidden until you move or do anything else. A cat holding the prize can’t hide: there’s a chicken sticking out from under the sofa.

Doing things in view

Walking, sitting, washing and staring are free, even right under a human’s nose — and so is going in or out through an entrance that needs no roll (unless you’re carrying the prize: Chapter 8). Helping another cat’s roll is never a roll of its own. Anything else you do while seen is a roll, even things that are usually simple, like picking something up. Hiding in view is always a Sneaky roll; for everything else, choose how:

  • Sneaky — do it without them noticing. Their Attention takes dice away, but a Clean Sneaky roll adds nothing to the Mood.
  • Savage — just do it, fast. Their Attention doesn’t matter, but they’ll notice.
  • Sweet — if what you’re doing is charming them.

The Mood

The house has one Mood with two meters, kept on the house tracker. Each meter fills up to the house’s Patience (4 to 8 in the houses in this book, a little more or less for three or five cats).

  • Broom fills when humans see cats being naughty. When it’s full, out comes the broom.
  • Cuddle fills when humans see cats being sweet. When it’s full, somebody gets picked up.

Mood steps

When a human sees your roll:

  • Clean or Scrape: 1 step. Sweet rolls fill Cuddle; Sneaky, Savage and Streetwise rolls fill Broom. A Sneaky Clean adds nothing — they never noticed.
  • Purrfect: nothing.
  • Flop: 2 steps on whichever meter is fuller (its own meter if they’re level). A flop makes their mood worse: if they’re already cross they get crosser, and if they’re already soppy they want to pick you up.
  • A kid who sees the roll — even a roll aimed at that kid — doubles Cuddle steps from it.
  • One roll changes the Mood once, however many humans saw it.
  • A meter never goes past full: steps beyond it are lost.

Some things add steps without a roll: at the start of a round, the Prize Clock finding the prize gone (Chapter 6) and a temptation given in to in view (Chapter 7); at the end of the house’s turn (Chapter 6, Finding it), +1 Broom when a human finds a mess you left, when humans find the prize disturbed, and when a Searching human can see a cat.

A roll that fills a meter still does what it did — the door is open, the foil is off — but the Shoo! or Scoop happens before the cat goes anywhere.

Shoo!

When Broom fills, the human who saw it (the one with the highest Attention; if they’re tied, the Storyteller picks) grabs the broom and chases that cat out through the nearest entrance it fits through that works this round (a front door only works in the rounds its house lists; Storyteller’s pick if two are as near). If that entrance is shut, the human opens it to put the cat out. Then they shut it behind the cat — unless it’s the house’s last entrance still open, which they leave open. The human stays in the room that entrance is in for the rest of this round (they answer no noise). When a meter fills without a roll (a Searching human spots you, the prize or a mess is found), the Storyteller picks which cat that human can see gets it. The cat is outside. It can come back in through any entrance that’s still open, next round at the earliest.

Scooped!

When Cuddle fills, the human who saw it (the one with the highest Attention; if they’re tied, the Storyteller picks) picks that cat up. The cat is Held (Chapter 9). If the roll that filled Cuddle was a Distract, the Scoop wins: that human picks the cat up and isn’t Distracted.

After a Shoo! or a Scoop

  • If the cat was carrying the prize, it drops it: the human puts it back where the Prize Clock last put it, and every step of that spot has to be done again. Eaten portions stay eaten. (A cat caught by the very roll that picked the prize up was carrying it.) If the cat wasn’t carrying it, the prize stays as it is.
  • That meter empties to 0.
  • Patience drops by 1 (never below 2). The house is getting fed up.
  • The other meter stays where it is — but if it’s now at or above the new Patience, it drops to one below. (One catch never sets off the other straight away.)
  • If a meter fills and no human with free hands can see a cat, it stays full until one can; then that human (the highest Attention; ties, the Storyteller) acts at once, and the Storyteller picks which cat they can see gets it. While it waits, steps on it are lost. If both meters are full and waiting, Broom goes first.
  • A held cat whose own roll fills Broom is put outside: the holder lets go and shoos it out, as above.

Reading the room

Because the meters fill separately, the gang can steer. If Broom is nearly full, be sweet for a while; if Cuddle is nearly full, keep the Charmer out of sight and let the Muscle work. Watch both meters, and remember that Patience only ever goes down.

Example

The Okafors’ house has Patience 7. Mrs Okafor is in the living room with the box. Velvet distracts her with a slow blink (Sweet, Clean): Cuddle 1. Pip, carrying the box with Biscuit and Mags past Tayo in the hall, rolls a Scrape: Broom 1. Later Mags flops a Sneaky roll in front of Tayo. The meters are level (1 and 1), so the Flop goes on its own meter: Broom +2, now 3. Tayo is a kid, but he only doubles Cuddle steps.
