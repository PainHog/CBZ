Grandpa Wendell’s Catnip Mouse

“It’s for Duchess. She deserves nice things.”

Read this aloud

Grandpa Wendell comes home from the pet shop with a paper bag, and through the paper you can smell it: catnip. A whole catnip mouse, red felt, with a string tail. His daughter June is in the kitchen putting the kettle on. In the front window, a large fluffy cat called Duchess watches you all with deep contempt.

{{SHEET}}

Grandpa Wendell’s house has a hall, a front room where Duchess rules, a kitchen with Duchess’s microchip cat flap, a cellar under the kitchen with a cracked window, and upstairs a spare bedroom, a landing and the bathroom. The mouse is small — one cat can carry it — but it has to get out of the house, and Duchess will want it too.

How it plays

  • Grab the mouse from the shopping bag in the hall before Grandpa gives it to Duchess at the start of round 4. After that you have to get past her.
  • Grandpa dozes. A sleeping Grandpa sees nothing — perfect for a quick snatch.
  • The robot vacuum tempts Hunters wherever it goes — and can be ridden.
  • Getting out is the hard part: plan the exit before you grab the mouse.
