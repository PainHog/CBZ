Chef Marchetti’s Salmon

The best fish on the street, in the hardest kitchen on the street.

Read this aloud

Chef Marchetti carries a whole salmon past Sam at the front door and straight through to her kitchen like a trophy, wrapped in paper from the fish market. From the utility room comes a deep, slow woof, and then the claws of a very big dog on a tiled floor. The kitchen lights go on, and they stay on. This is the big score, and everybody on the wall knows it.

{{SHEET}}

Chef Marchetti cooks for a living, and she hardly ever leaves her kitchen while there’s a fish on the island. Her partner Sam wanders the house. Their dog Brutus is enormous and not the least bit soft. Upstairs there’s a balcony door up the tree, a skylight propped open, a bedroom and a study.

How it plays

  • Chef Marchetti is busy: no noise will lure her out of the kitchen. She has Attention 2. You will have to work under her nose — or distract her, which gets harder every time.
  • Brutus can’t be befriended. Drive him off (rating 3: bring your Muscle) or stay out of his way.
  • Brutus’s dog door is a heavy flap: every cat through it makes a noise, right in his room. The way in from upstairs is long but quiet.
  • The fish is a lot of portions, and every one is a roll under the Chef’s nose. Carrying it out past Brutus is fewer rolls, but a carrier can’t hide and every haul can fail; most gangs do better eating it. This house keeps more cats than any other: keep the dog door open for the Chonky ones (the skylight is Tight).
