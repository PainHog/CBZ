# Book art

All art is hand-built SVG, generated by the scripts in `book/tools/art-gen/` and
inlined at build time wherever the chapters say `<figure class="art" data-art="NAME">`.
Every piece must pass `ART-CHECKLIST.md` (including the object-table audit), and every
rendered piece is looked at (`node book/tools/preview-art.mjs <outDir> [name | name@x,y,w,h]`;
the `@` form renders a zoomed crop) before it is called done. Every SVG has a `viewBox` and **no**
fixed width or height; the CSS sizes it. No `<image>`, `<script>`, filters such as `feTurbulence`,
or external links. Rebuild with `node book/tools/art-gen/scenes/build.mjs [names]`.

## House style
- Night street outside, warm lamplight inside (DESIGN.md Q10). Flat shapes with thick blue-black ink
  outlines (3–3.5 px at the art's native scale), soft contact shadows, no gradients except a lamp glow.
- **Comical and light** (the author, 2026-10-21): the gang are cat burglars who love shiny things, played
  for laughs. Tough looks that don't quite work (a lopsided squint, a crooked grin with one fang),
  kerchiefs a bit too big, bling a bit too heavy, daft tattoos, swagger and slapstick. Never menacing.
  The chasers (the warden, the dogs) are comic too.
- **One silhouette** (the author, 2026-10-21): a side-view cat or dog is one continuous shape. Body, neck,
  head, near legs and tail are inked as a single outline, with no line where a leg, the neck or the tail
  joins the body; legs are single tapered shapes ending in small round paws; the far legs and far ear sit
  behind in the deeper shade. Running and leaping cats keep a body about 2.5–3 heads long (a kitten's
  shorter): the stretch goes into the legs and the tail.
- Cats are drawn only by `tools/art-gen/cats.mjs`, residents only by `residents.mjs`, so each keeps
  one design.
- Fonts in the art: Alegreya Sans (labels, maps, captions), Fraunces (titles, BROOM / CUDDLE, the
  parrot's PARTY!), Rubik Spray Paint (the gang's bubble-letter throw-ups). Never a generic family alone.

## Scale (one scale for the whole book)
In the interior vignettes (900×300, floor at y = 262; `kit.mjs` `SCALE`):

| Thing | Size |
|---|---|
| A gang cat | `s = 0.26` (a sitting adult is about 74 tall) |
| Pip | 0.82× an adult (she scales herself by her look) |
| Pom-Pom | 1.2× an adult (large and fluffy) |
| Brutus | about twice a cat's height at the shoulder (`s = 0.66`) |
| Kitchen counter | 150 tall |
| Table | 120 tall; chair seat 86 |
| Door | 330 tall: its top always runs out of the frame |
| People | shown from the hips down only, cropped by the top edge, so no visible part of a person is ever taller than a door |

Outdoors (the street, the fence, the cover) a whole person may show; then the cats stay readable and
the person stays shorter than his own front door. Spots are drawn at the vignettes' scale and then
scaled up as a whole. Maps have their own single scale (below).

## Graffiti (the author, 2026-10-21)
- **Outdoors only:** garden walls, fences, wheelie bins, the alley wall, a chimney, the warden's van.
  Never inside anyone's home.
- **What:** the gang's cat-head 9 tag (`gangTag`); abstract marks with no letters (`tagMark`: loop, crown,
  star, arrow, paw prints, a fish); bubble-letter throw-ups of only the short real words MEOW, 9 LIVES
  and NLG (`throwUp` refuses anything else). No fake writing.
- **Colours:** window-glow cream, pale blue, light, dusk blue; never Broom red-orange or Cuddle pink.
- **The rivals:** a made-up rival crew's tag, a scruffy fish-skeleton scrawl in dull green
  (`rivalTag`), appears once, with the gang's tag sprayed over it (`spot-rivals`).

### The gang's tag
- `orn-tag` (140×110): the sprayed tag with overspray and drips, for the part pages, the credits page and

- `tag-small` (140×92): the same cat's head, 9 and whiskers as bold, clean strokes (no overspray, no

## Maps (plan view)
- One scale for every map: each map's viewBox is 1028 units wide and prints at the full figure width,
  7.14in (514pt), so one grid square (72 units) is 36pt, half an inch, on every map. If a map is ever
  printed narrower, its scale changes with it.
- Labels in Alegreya Sans (room names in Fraunces); the smallest text is 15 units, 7.5pt in print.
- Each map is drawn from the same data as the simulator (`sim/houses.mjs`, read only); regenerate the
  maps whenever that data changes.
- Doors: an open doorway is a gap in the wall. A closed door is drawn as its leaf shut across the
  opening with a dashed swing (the legend says so). Swings never cross a label.
- Entrances are numbered markers on the walls, listed under ENTRANCES; the star marks where the prize is
  put down, and in which round; THE DEN is in Cuddle pink.
