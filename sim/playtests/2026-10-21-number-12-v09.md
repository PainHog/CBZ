# Playtest: The Chicken at Number 12 (Easy), gang of 4 rolled cats, rules v0.9, real dice

*2026-10-21 · Played strictly by the rulebook source: Chapters 1–14 (`book/src/chapters/11-ch01` to `33-ch14`, including the new Chapter 9 getaway and its "Cred: showing off" section), the house chapter `41-ch15-number-12.html`, the map `book/art/map-number-12.svg` and the three sheets (`50`, `51`, `52`). One agent played the Storyteller and all four players. The players played to win and to score Cred. The simulator, the Foundry module, the design notes and the review log were not read; only earlier playtest reports were used, for format. Every die came from `crypto.randomInt` through a logging script, which logged each roll with its purpose as it was made. Every roll is listed in order in the Dice appendix.*

## Summary

- **Outcome: Clean Getaway.** All 5 portions of the chicken were eaten inside, nobody was pinched, and all four cats reached the hideout in getaway round 4. The house part ended at the end of round 9, three rounds before Lights Out (end of round 12). The chicken was finished in round 6, well before it would have gone (start of round 10). The line:
  - **Round 1.** All four came in through the cat flap. Sully opened the utility door with a Purrfect. Joe rolled in the catnip on purpose: he was **on the nip**, with 1 Cred. The flap's clack drew Mr Pell into the utility, chicken and all.
  - **Round 2.** The Clock put the chicken on the kitchen counter, with Mr Pell beside it and deaf to noise. Joe, on the nip, couldn't resist a spider. Pearl befriended Pudding (Cuddle 1).
  - **Round 3.** Pearl Distracted Mr Pell (Cuddle 2). Joe climbed up to the chicken and Sully peeled the foil. Mags popped out of the cat flap, and the clack pulled Mr Pell off the open chicken before he could find it.
  - **Round 4.** Joe crashed the umbrella stand in the hall as a lure (a mess: Broom 1). Pearl and Sully each ate a portion with a quiet Clean.
  - **Round 5.** Joe tagged the loo over the rival gang's tag (4 Cred). Pearl and Mags ate. Sully Flopped twice. The noise brought Mr Pell back, and he found the chicken (Broom 2) and started **Searching**.
  - **Round 6.** Pearl ate the last portion in front of him (Broom 3). Joe tagged the hall. Mr Pell spotted the eaters: **Shoo!** Pearl was swept out of the kitchen window (Patience 3).
  - **Round 7.** Sully left by the flap. Mags tagged the utility and Joe tagged the living room. The clack drew Mr Pell to the utility, where he spotted Mags and found her tag (Broom 2/3).
  - **Round 8.** Mags left. Joe hid in the hall.
  - **Round 9.** Joe tagged the fridge right under Mr Pell's nose (a Sneaky Clean: 4 Cred). Mr Pell spotted him: **Shoo!** Joe went out of the window (Patience 2). No free cat was inside and the chicken was eaten, so the house part was over.
  - **The getaway (4 rounds).** The cats went in two pairs each round: 8 rolls and no Flops. Sully led his pair past the Bin Lid Boys with a Clean (1 Cred). Mags Pushed a Clean on the alley wall into a Purrfect (1 Cred). The warden never got closer than one space behind.
- **End Mood:** Broom 1/2, Cuddle 1/2, Patience 2 (from 4, after two Shoos). Nobody was Scooped, Held, shut in or pinched.
- **Cred:** **Midnight Joe 13**, Alley Mags 2, Midnight Sully 1, Alley Pearl 0. **The Cat's Whiskers: Midnight Joe.** In a campaign this night would be worth **2 Rep**: 1 for the Clean Getaway, and 1 for 5 or more Cred, not Busted, under either reading of W-09.
- **Rolls:**
  - **Cats in the house:** 12 rolls (34 dice) and 1 Push. Results: 1 Purrfect, 4 Clean, 6 Scrape, 1 Flop (Pushed, still a Flop).
  - **Cats on the getaway:** 8 rolls (29 dice) and 3 Pushes, all Pushes for a Purrfect on a Clean. Final results: 6 Clean, 1 Scrape, 1 Purrfect (after a Push), 0 Flops.
  - **Storyteller:** 10 rolls: 9 for Pudding's routine and 1 for Mr Pell's. Mr Pell rolled his routine only once, in round 8. In every other round he answered a noise, stayed with the chicken for the Clock, or was hunting as a Searching human.
  - **Temptations:** 5, all chosen by the Storyteller. 1 was given in to (Joe, on the nip, who couldn't resist) and 4 were resisted.
  - **Making the gang:** 32 roll events. **Drill G:** 11. **Drill H:** 19. **In all:** 96 dice-log entries.
- **Cat-turns in the house: 30.** **25 were active:** a roll, a help, a temptation, a tag, a lure or the nip. **5 were idle:** walking in, waiting or walking out.
- **Two supplementary getaway drills** (real dice, set positions) tested the loot rules the main line skipped, because there the food was all eaten:
  - **Drill G**, carriers as helpers: a Clean Getaway with all 3 pieces. The loot penalty never applied.
  - **Drill H**, everyone running on their own: a Messy Getaway. One piece was lost to the warden and one was nicked by the rivals. A single Flop let the warden corner the whole gang. All four dodged and skipped the alley wall.
- **Issues: 27** (0 blockers, 10 major, 17 minor). **Top 5:**
  1. **W-01 (major):** the rival gang's tag is in the Den, and "A cat in the Den is Shut In". Read literally, a cat that goes in to tag over the rivals gets shut in, and is pinched if it's still there at the end.
  2. **W-02 (major):** the getaway route's spaces aren't defined. The tracker has six columns (Start, 1, 2, 3, 4, Hideout), but four obstacles give five positions. Where the warden arrives, and where dropped loot lies relative to the rivals, both depend on this.
  3. **W-03 (major):** a dodge that works "bolts on to the space just ahead of the warden", so it can carry a cat past obstacles it never crossed. In Drill H one Flop sent the warden two spaces, which cornered all four cats, and the dodges moved the whole gang past the alley wall.
  4. **W-06 (major):** the nip doubles tags that need no roll when unseen. Joe took the nip in round 1 for free and scored 13 Cred, mostly from tags nobody ever found. Nobody else scored more than 2, so the Cat's Whiskers was settled in round 1.
  5. **W-04 (major):** the loot penalty only hits the cat who rolls. Carriers who help an empty-mouthed leader ride free: Drill G made 5-dice rolls with every piece aboard.
- **Headline feel:** the house part was a proper little heist. A plan worked step by step, 25 of 30 cat-turns did something, and Cred gave rounds 6–9 a fun "show off on the way out" act that the earlier Number 12 runs didn't have. Two things fell flat:
  - **The Cred race was over in round 1.** Pearl befriended the dog, Distracted Mr Pell and ate 3 of the 5 portions, and she scored 0 Cred.
  - **The getaway had no stakes.** Eating everything inside, which the book recommends, leaves nothing to carry. Its 8 rolls only mattered for Cred.

### Drill G: a getaway with loot, carriers helping (supplementary, real dice)

**This is not the robbery's result.** The main line carried no loot, so I set up a position that does.
- **The chicken was hauled out with 3 portions left** (2 were eaten inside), so the loot is 3 pieces.
- **Nobody was Shooed or Scooped,** so the warden arrives at the end of round 2.

## Feel notes

- **Was it fun?** Yes. The house part played like a proper little heist.
  - **The plan:** befriend the dog while the human is deaf. Then Distract, climb and peel in one round, with a lure to pull him off the open chicken. Then eat it in two rounds.
  - **The comic moments:** Mr Pell carrying the chicken into the utility to see what clacked; Joe, high on catnip, chasing a spider round the washing machine; "OI! That's my supper!"; Pearl swept out of the window with a belly full of chicken; Joe yowling while he tagged the fridge right under Mr Pell's nose.
  - **The numbers:** 25 of 30 cat-turns did something, against 12 of 16 in the earlier v0.8 run, which ended in round 4.
- **Cred gave the night a third act.** In the earlier runs the robbery ended the moment the prize was out. Here rounds 6–9 were all about showing off on the way out, and the players loved deciding whether one more tag was worth the broom. "Smart gangs tag on the way out" happened naturally.
- **But the Cred race was over in round 1 (W-06).** The nip doubled tags that cost nothing. Pearl befriended the dog, Distracted Mr Pell and ate three of the five portions, and she ended with 0 Cred. Sully opened the door with a Purrfect, peeled the foil and ate a portion, and ended with 1. Three of the four players had nothing to brag about, and the Cat's Whiskers was never in doubt.
- **The Shoo! was the best way out.** Two of the four cats left by the broom, and for Joe it was the plan: tag in view, get spotted, get swept out of the window. That fits the tone ("the broom only ever sweeps a cat out of the door"). Once the food is eaten, though, a Shoo costs only the warden's early arrival, and that costs nothing when there's no loot.
- **The getaway was a formality in the main line (W-24).** The chicken was all eaten, so there was nothing to carry, and the warden never got within a space. The only tension was the Pushes for a Purrfect. Drill H shows the getaway can be a real chase, with a piece bouncing into the gutter, the Bin Lid Boys nicking one, and the warden's net swishing past four ears. It also shows a Flop helping the gang (W-03).
- **Temptations bit, but only on the cats without the nip.** Pearl and Sully resisted down to 0 Cattitude, which cost Sully a Push and left Pearl with none for the getaway. Joe, on the nip, "couldn't resist", but the Storyteller only had a free moment to tempt him (Mr Pell deaf in round 2), and it gave Joe a Cattitude.
- **The fuller-meter Flop was the scariest rule.** From round 3 Cuddle sat at 2 of 4. Any Flop in view (even a Savage tag) would have gone on Cuddle and got a cat Scooped. The gang made no risky roll in front of Mr Pell until Broom caught up. That's good tension, but a little odd to picture: a cat clawing the sofa gets a cuddle.
- **Tone:** nothing broke the light, comic feel. The warden, the van and the pound stay cartoonish. A rival gang to show up is a great hook for kids.
- **How lucky was it?**
  - The gang rolled no Flop in front of Mr Pell. The last portion (4 dice, a 1-in-16 Flop, which would have been a Shoo!) came up a Scrape.
  - The getaway had no Flops in 8 rolls of 3–5 dice (about 0.6 expected).
  - The house's dice were kind: Pudding stayed in the kitchen for rounds 1–8, and Mr Pell's one routine roll came up the kitchen.
- **For an 8-year-old:**
  - Where do I put my counter on the getaway, and where is the warden? (W-02)
  - Why did dodging send me over the wall? (W-03)
  - "I yowled, but he didn't notice?" (W-07)
  - Can I get shut in the loo when I go to tag it? (W-01)
  - Are the Bin Lid Boys us? (W-27)
