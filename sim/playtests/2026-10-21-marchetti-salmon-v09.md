# Playtest: Chef Marchetti's Salmon (Hard), five cats, rules v0.9 (the getaway and Cred), real dice

*2026-10-21 · One Storyteller and five players, all played by one agent, strictly by the rulebook source: Chapters 1–14 (`book/src/chapters/11-ch01` to `33-ch14`, including the new Chapter 9, The Getaway, with its "Cred: showing off" section), the generated house chapter (Chapter 19), the floor plan (`book/art/map-marchetti-salmon.svg`) and the three sheets. Nothing else was read except the earlier playtest reports, for format. The gang is the ready-made Bin Lid Gang from Chapter 3, all five cats, on the house's five-cat numbers. The Storyteller played the house by the house chapter and Chapter 12 and tempted meanly, by the rules. The players played to win, and to show off for Cred. Every die came from Node's `crypto.randomInt` through a logging script that wrote each roll and its purpose before the result was used. All 68 rolls are in the Dice appendix, including two what-if getaways rolled to exercise the loot and warden rules that the main line never reached.*

## Summary

- **Outcome: Clean Getaway.**
  - All 7 portions of the salmon were eaten in the house: Biscuit 2, Mags 2, Pip 2, Tilly 1.
  - Every cat was out by the end of round 7 of a possible 11, and all five reached the hideout in 4 getaway rounds.
  - Nobody was pinched, Scooped, Held or shut in. Tilly was Shooed once (round 6).
- **Round by round:**
  1. Velvet said hello to Sam at the front door (Purrfect). Biscuit, Mags and Pip came in through the noisy dog door, and Biscuit drove Brutus off (Purrfect). Tilly climbed in through the skylight. The flap's noise drew Sam into the utility room.
  2. Biscuit shoved the kitchen door open in front of Sam (Broom 1). Velvet tagged the empty hall. Tilly rolled in Sam's catnip plant upstairs and was **on the nip**.
  3. Velvet distracted the Chef while Mags climbed up to the salmon and Biscuit tore the paper off. Tilly tagged the study (doubled). The noise brought Sam in; he found the open salmon (Broom 2) and started Searching. The Chef's one-in-six routine took her to the hall, where she found Velvet's tag (Broom 3).
  4. Velvet distracted Searching Sam, and Biscuit, Mags and Pip ate 3 portions. Everyone slipped into the hall, and Tilly tagged the landing (doubled). The Chef came back and found the fish (Broom 4, Searching). Brutus wandered into the hall, right among the cats.
  5. Biscuit drove Brutus off again. The noise pulled Sam into the hall, so the kitchen had only the Chef. Velvet distracted her a second time and Tilly ate portion 4, which is already Messy if everyone gets home. Spotted: Broom 5.
  6. Velvet's third distraction (1 die) worked. Biscuit (Purrfect), Mags (a Flop Pushed into a Purrfect) and Pip ate the last 3 portions. The gang ducked into the utility room, the Searching Chef followed and spotted them, and Broom filled: **Shoo!** Tilly was swept out of the dog door, which was shut behind her (Broom 0, Patience 5).
  7. Pip reopened the dog door under the Chef's nose. Biscuit tagged over the Fishbone Crew's mark in front of her (3 Cred), and all four walked out.
  - **Getaway:** round 1, over the restaurant yard (Biscuit Purrfect); the warden jumps out. Round 2, past the fishmonger's cat. Round 3, over the market wall (Tilly's group Purrfect, right past the rivals). Round 4, across the market street, and home.
- **End Mood:** Broom 3/5, Cuddle 4/5, Patience 5 (from 6; one Shoo). Broom was full once (the Shoo). The last Broom step was the rivals' tag being found after the gang had gone.
- **Cattitude** at the end of the house part: Tilly 2, Biscuit 1, Velvet 2, Mags 2, Pip 1. After the getaway: Tilly 3, Biscuit 2, the rest the same.
- **Rolls:**
  - **Cats in the house: 21 log entries** (20 rolls and 1 Push): 3 Purrfect, 3 Clean, 13 Scrape, 1 Flop. The Flop was Pushed into a Purrfect.
  - **Storyteller: 8** (3 Chef routines, 1 Sam routine, 1 Brutus routine, 3 vacuum). Sam's routine was rolled only once: noise or Searching moved him every other round.
  - **Main getaway: 10 rolls:** 2 Purrfect, 3 Clean, 5 Scrape, 0 Flop.
  - **What-if getaways: 29 entries** (Branch G 10, Branch W 19).
  - **In all: 68 entries.** Temptations were chosen by the Storyteller, never rolled.
- **Cat-turns in the house: 35** (5 cats × 7 rounds). 20 rolled, 10 helped, 4 were no-roll Actions (3 tags and the catnip), and 1 was spent outside (Tilly in round 7). None was idle.
- **Temptations:** 5 offered and 5 resisted. In round 5 none was possible (W-07). Nobody gave in all night.
- **Cred:** **Tilly 7**, Biscuit 4, Velvet 1, Mags 0, Pip 0 (gang 12). **The Cat's Whiskers: Two-Bin Tilly.** In a campaign the night is worth 2 Rep: 1 for a Clean Getaway, and 1 for 5 or more Cred under either reading of W-08.
- **Issues: 27** (0 blockers, 8 major, 19 minor). **Top 5:**
  1. **W-01 (major): the outcome has no fixed total.** "All of the loot is home or eaten" counts only what came out plus what was eaten. Read literally, a gang that eats one portion and leaves the rest inside scores a Clean Getaway. Fix: count the whole prize in pieces, and round half up (4 of 7).
  2. **W-02 (major): a dodge carries a cat over obstacles it never crossed.** In Branch W, Velvet Flopped her way along and two dodges took her from the start to space 3. Her Sweet dodge (4 dice) was the easiest roll she had all night. Fix: the warden stops at the first cat, and a dodge sends him back a space instead of moving the cat on.
  3. **W-03 (major): only the roller loses a die for loot.** A sensible gang hands every piece to its helpers and runs with no penalty at all. Branch G carried 3 pieces home without losing a single die. Fix: a group loses 1 die if anyone in it is carrying.
  4. **W-06 (major): the nip doubles Cred from no-roll tags.** Tilly scored 4 of her 7 Cred by tagging two empty upstairs rooms: no roll, no noise, no risk. The nip's two costs (noisy rolls, can't resist) never bit. Fix: on the nip, tagging is always a roll.
  5. **W-04 (major): the route's spaces and the warden's start aren't defined.** The tracker has six columns for the five places a cat can be. It is unclear whether the warden "beside the house" is on the start, which decides whether a cat who Flops the first obstacle is cornered at once. Fix: make each obstacle a space and put the warden one space behind the first.
- **What-if getaways (rolled for real; not the result):**
  - **Branch G**, 3 pieces with sensible groups: all home, Clean. The loot cost no dice, because the carriers only ever helped (W-03).
  - **Branch W**, 3 pieces with a greedy every-cat-for-itself table: Velvet was cornered twice and dodged twice, and one piece was lost to the warden, so Messy. Nobody was pinched; it was close (a 1-die dodge saved by her last Cattitude).

### W-01 (major) The outcome has no fixed total
- **Where:** Chapter 9, *How did it go?*; also *The loot*, and the Quick Reference's *How it went*.
- **Passage:** "Count the loot: the pieces that made it home, plus any portions eaten in the house." The table: "Clean Getaway — All of the loot is home or eaten, and nobody was pinched. Messy Getaway — At least half of it — or all of it, but somebody was pinched. Busted — Less than half." And *The loot*: "Whatever the gang got out of the house is loot".
- **The problem:** "all of the loot" is never measured against the whole prize. Read literally, loot is only what came out of the house. So a gang that eats one portion, leaves six on the island and walks out has "all of the loot … home or eaten": a Clean Getaway. Also, half of 7 portions is 3.5.
- **What I ruled:** the total is the whole prize in pieces: 7 portions here. Messy needs 4 of 7 (half, rounded up). It didn't decide this game (7 of 7 were eaten), but it decides any game where the prize is left inside.
- **Suggested fix:** replace the paragraph and table with: "Count the prize in pieces: a food prize is all of its portions, and anything else is 1, 2 or 3 pieces by size. Each piece got home, was eaten in the house, or was lost. **Clean Getaway:** every piece is home or eaten, and nobody was pinched. **Messy Getaway:** at least half of the pieces (round up: 4 of 7), or every piece but somebody was pinched. **Busted:** fewer than half." In the Quick Reference: "Count the whole prize in pieces (portions, or 1/2/3 by size)…"

### W-02 (major) A dodge carries a cat over obstacles it never crossed

### W-23 (minor) Does driving off the fishmonger's cat clear it?
- **Where:** Chapter 9, *Running with the loot*; Chapter 11, *Beat it*.
- **Passage:** "An obstacle with a dog or a cat on it lists its rating, and that takes dice off the roll as usual (Chapter 11)." Chapter 11: "Savage drives it off (it hides for 3 rounds, counting this one)."
- **What I ruled:** each group rolls on its own. In getaway round 2 Biscuit's group saw it off with Savage, and Mags's group slipped past it with Sneaky, in the same round.
- **Suggested fix:** "On the route a pet is an obstacle: every cat or group rolls to get past it, and beating it only gets that group past."

### W-24 (minor) Leading past the rivals: once, or once per group, and does it stack?
- **Where:** Chapter 9, the Cred table and *The rivals*.
- **Passage:** the table: "Lead the gang past the rivals on the getaway with a Clean or a Purrfect — 1". The text: "the cat who leads a group over it with a Clean or a Purrfect scores 1."
- **What I ruled:** every group's leader can score it, and it stacks with the Purrfect leap. Tilly scored 2 for one roll.
- **Suggested fix:** in the table: "Lead a group past the rivals on the getaway with a Clean or a Purrfect (each group’s leader)". Add "(this and a Purrfect leap both count)".

### W-25 (minor) Can a getaway cat wait?
- **Where:** Chapter 9, *A getaway round*, step 1.
- **Passage:** "It tries to get over the next obstacle … — or helps a cat on its space …, or picks up a piece of loot dropped on its space instead of going on."
- **What I ruled:** a cat must do one of the three. In Branch W, Velvet, alone with 0 dice, had to roll and Flop, which also moved the warden 2.
- **Suggested fix:** say which. Either "Every cat must do one of these" or "…or waits where it is (no roll)".

### W-26 (minor) Does a Flop saved by a Push speed the warden up?
- **Where:** Chapter 9, *A getaway round*, step 2.
- **Passage:** "two if any cat Flopped this round."
- **What I ruled:** no. Only the final reading counts (Branch G: Biscuit at the wall; Branch W: Biscuit, Tilly and Mags).
- **Suggested fix:** "two if any cat’s roll ended in a Flop this round (a Flop saved by a Push doesn’t count)".

### W-27 (minor) One tag example reads as real damage
- **Where:** Chapter 9, the Cred table.
- **Passage:** "Tag a room: leave the gang’s mark (a cheek-rub on the telly, claw marks down the sofa, a muddy paw-print tag on the fridge)."
- **The problem:** everything else in the book is slapstick that cleans up. "Claw marks down the sofa" is the one thing a real family would be upset about, and the only example with no fix in sight.
- **Suggested fix:** "(a cheek-rub on the telly, a tuft of fur on the best cushion, a muddy paw-print tag on the fridge)".

## Feel notes

- **Was it fun? The house part, very.** The comic beats came straight out of the rules:
  - Velvet's double-six hello at the door.
  - Brutus skidding away from Biscuit twice.
  - Tilly rolling about in Sam's catnip plant upstairs, yowling.
  - The Chef's one-in-six trip to the hall, where she found a paw-print on the coat stand instead of noticing her fish.
  - The Searching Chef following the gang into the utility room and sweeping Tilly out of the dog door.
  - Best of all, Biscuit putting the Bin Lid Gang's mark over the Fishbone Crew's, Savage, while the Chef watched. **Cred works as a story engine.** It gave the players something to want besides the fish, and it gave the night's best scene.
- **Was it Hard in a good way? Yes.**
  - With S-01 to S-03 fixed, the house is the hardest kitchen on the street.
  - From round 4 both humans were Searching beside the fish, and every portion was a roll. From round 5 Broom sat one short of full.
  - The gang needed three distractions of the Chef (2, 2 and then 1 die), two drive-offs and a Pushed Purrfect.
  - It ended with the Clean only because 13 of the 20 rolls were Scrapes: plenty of noise, but only one Flop.
- **Temptations were a tax, not a story.** 5 were offered and 5 resisted. The mean Storyteller always picked the cat whose Action the plan needed, so giving in was never the right call. Five cats with 2 Cattitude each could always afford to say no. The designer note promises "pick the best moment to say yes"; there wasn't one.
- **What wasn't fun:**
  - **Tilly's upstairs spree** (W-06). Two rounds of "I tag the landing" with no roll, no risk and nobody else involved, worth more Cred than anything done under a human's nose.
  - **The main-line getaway.** With all the salmon eaten there was nothing to carry, and with no Flops the warden never got within two spaces. It was four rounds and ten rolls with no decision that mattered beyond who leads for Cred. The Storyteller's *Make it a chase* box had nothing to work with.
  - The getaway only came alive in the branches, once there was loot and a Flop: Velvet's piece bouncing into the gutter and the warden's net swishing past her ear. Even there, the dodge rule (W-02) made getting cornered a shortcut.
- **The new rules:**
  - **Tagging, the rivals' tag and the rivals on the route** all worked and all made story. "Smart gangs tag on the way out" was exactly the right advice: the gang's one tag made early (the hall) was found by the Chef for Broom +1.
  - **The catnip** is a great idea. It sits upstairs in the Den, though, so only a Lean climber reaches it early, and its downsides never bit (W-06).
  - **Shiny things** gave the Storyteller temptations in rooms that list none (Pip in the utility room), as intended.
  - **Pinched cats** never happened. Branch W came within one die of it.
  - **The getaway's loot rules** are clear to play, but helper-carrying removes their cost (W-03).
- **The robot vacuum** wandered between the living room and the hall all night, and never once shared a room with a Hunter at the start of a round. It was scenery again.
- **Tone:** light and cartoonish throughout. The broom, the warden's net, "I'm somebody's cat!" and the pound all stay slapstick. The only jarring note is "claw marks down the sofa" (W-27).
- **What might confuse an 8-year-old:**
  - Where the warden starts, and what a "space" is (W-04).
  - Why getting cornered moved Velvet ahead of her friends (W-02).
  - Why nobody can be tempted while the dog is in the room (W-07).
