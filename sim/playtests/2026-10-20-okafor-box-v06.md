# Verification playtest: The Okafors' Big Box (Standard), rules v0.6, real dice

*2026-10-20. One Storyteller and four players, all played by one agent, strictly by the rulebook chapters (`book/src/chapters/21–27`, `12-ch02-your-cat.html`, `31-ch11-storyteller.html`, `42-ch15-okafor-box.html`) and the house's map. `docs/CORE-RULES.md` v0.6 and `book/REVIEW.md` were read for cross-checking only; the chapters won every disagreement. Every die came from Node's `crypto.randomInt`, through a private logging script, one numbered line per roll, logged before use. All 60 are in the Dice appendix.*

## Summary

- **Outcome: Clean Getaway in round 8.** Nobody was Held, shut in or adopted. One Shoo! happened, on purpose (see below). The box would have gone at the start of round 13.
- **End Mood:** Broom 0/7, Cuddle 1/7, Patience 7 (it started at 8; the Shoo! took 1).
- **The 0-roll win is gone.** Hauling made the box cost **3 cat rolls** (one per room: hall → kitchen → garage → out), plus 1 Push. The Storyteller rolled 9 times. Making the gang took 33 rolls.
- **But the gang still won with a no-roll trick at the decisive moment.** In round 7, Broom stood at 7/8, Searching Mrs Okafor was standing over the box in the garage, and the last haul was a coin flip (4 dice: 52% Clean, and anything else fills Broom and sends the box back to the hall). Instead, the free cat (Tilly) walked into the kitchen beside the awake dog and did nothing. The dog barked. Mrs Okafor, though Searching, **answered the noise** and left the box. At the end of the house's turn she could see Tilly, so Broom filled, and **she Shooed Tilly** out of the dog door. Broom emptied to 0, and Tilly was outside, where she had to end up anyway. Next round the haul out was unseen (5 dice, 97% to work) and Clean.
- **What-if branch B (Searching humans ignore noise; same position after round 6): Busted.** Two hauls in Mrs Okafor's view at nearly full Broom both rolled Scrapes. Each filled Broom, Shooed the leader, sent the box back to the hall and shut the garage side door, its only way out. The box was gone at the start of round 13.
- **Cat-turns (main line):** 31 (4 cats × 7 rounds, plus 3 in round 8; Tilly was outside). **9 idle** (rounds 2, 3 and 7: the three carriers waiting). 22 active. 9 of the active ones were on a roll (3 hauls × 1 roller + 2 helpers). Pushes: 1. Faces: 3 Scrapes (one of them the Push), 1 Clean, 0 Flops, 0 Purrfects.
- **Issues: 18** (0 blockers, 5 major, 13 minor).
- **The 20 earlier issues (V-01 to V-20):** 16 fixed. 3 fixed but with a new gap the fix opened (V-03, V-05 and V-15). 1 only partly fixed (V-02: the decoy is dead, but noise still steers Searchers).

**Top 5 issues**
1. **V6-02 (major): noise still pulls a Searching human off the prize.** Noise comes before routines, and nothing exempts Searchers. A bark (or any noise) next door drags them away from the box for a round. This decided the game.
2. **V6-01 (major): a Shoo! of the free cat is a free reset.** When Broom is one short, park the cat that isn't carrying in a Searcher's sight. The Shoo! hits that cat, empties Broom to 0, costs 1 Patience, and puts the cat outside, where it wanted to be.
3. **V6-05 (major): is a box held by cats in the Clock's room "where the Clock last put it"?** I said no: held means not in its place (the Distract text says "if the prize has been touched, they'll notice"). It decided round 5.
4. **V6-06 (major): may carriers haul out of an awake dog's room, or must they deal with the dog first?** I let hauling count as "leaving". The other reading costs a seen Savage roll and a whole round, and decided round 6.
5. **V6-04 (major): where is a haul's noise, in the room it left or the room it entered?** I used the room it ends up in. It decided which human came where in round 5.

**Dominant line now:**
- Enter by the dog door and hide.
- Lure the owner out of the hall (no roll, +1 Broom for the mess).
- Grab the box when the hall is empty.
- Haul it room by room (3 rolls). Use the free cat to split the humans with noise.
- When the last haul would be seen at nearly full Broom, let the dog bark the Searcher away and let the free cat take the Shoo!.

### What-if branch B: Searching humans ignore noise (not the result of this playtest)
**Why:** the main line was decided by V6-01 and V6-02. This branch tests my suggested fix, from exactly the end of round 6 (Broom 7/8, box in the garage, Mrs Okafor watching it, Tilly hidden in the hall). Fresh dice: #47–60.

  - Without the noise lure and the free-cat Shoo!, this gang didn't recover from one bad flip.
  - Better play was available: keep Tilly (Sweet 4 dice) free, and Distract the Searcher right before the last haul. My branch B gang wasted her as a carrier. Even so, the swing on one Scrape is large.

## Feel notes

- **Was it fun?**
  - Much more than v0.5. The three hauls rolled three Scrapes in a row (one of them a Push), and every one mattered.
  - They woke the dog, drew both humans and pushed Broom from 2 to 7 in two rounds.
  - Round 7 was a real puzzle, with the whole table leaning over the map. The trick the gang found is funny: getting your own cat chased out with a broom on purpose. But it's an exploit (V6-01, V6-02).
- **Was waiting still dominant?**
  - **Not as a way to win:** three hauls are three rolls whatever the gang does.
  - **But waiting was still free whenever the gang chose to:** 9 of 31 cat-turns were idle (R2, R3 and R7, the carriers each time).
  - Waiting in the kitchen cost one resist in the whole game. "Never the same thing twice" meant the kitchen could tempt this gang only once.
  - The R5 Clock gave the waiting a deadline, and that worked well. The gang had to have the box off the floor by round 5.
- **Did hauling add tension? Yes, the best change in v0.6.**
  - Every haul is a roll next to a sleeping dog, so the plan became how to make the noise land where it hurts least.
  - Hauling turned Scrapes into a resource problem: noise placement, lures to split the humans, and Pushes.
  - Weak spots:
    - Where the noise lands is undefined (V6-04).
    - The dog's Charge clashes with hauling (V6-06).
    - With three Lean carriers, the pool is fixed at 5 (4 in view), so Cattitude is useless for the roll itself and only buys Pushes and resists.
- **Did Searching work as the house's tool or the gang's? Mostly the house's, at last.**
  - Mrs Okafor shadowed the box room by room and charged +1 Broom every round she saw the carriers (R5, R6, plus the R7 Spotted).
  - The Spotted tick plus seen hauls is a strong clock: Broom went 2 → 7 in two rounds.
  - Branch B shows it can beat the gang outright.
  - **But at the decisive moment, noise turned it back into the gang's tool.** A Searcher still answers noise, and a Shoo! that fills on the free cat empties the meter.
- **Temptations:**
  - 6 offered: 1 given in (Tilly's lap fuss, which is still pure profit for a Lap Cat) and 5 resisted.
  - Every carrier temptation was resisted, because giving in drops a Large box.
  - So by round 8 all three carriers had 0 Cattitude, and every later Push could only be paid in Broom. That is good pressure, and exactly the Storyteller's lever the rules intend.
  - "The tempted cat goes first" also pulls the joint haul to the front of the turn (V6-07).
- **The dog:**
  - Woke on the first haul and barked the same turn.
  - Then served as the gang's free lure (V6-03).
  - Driving off, befriending and slipping past went unused again (three playtests running).
- **The Shoo! reset:** the pressure valve is too strong. One Shoo! costs 1 Patience and an entrance, but empties a 7/8 meter, and the free cat "pays" with an exit it wanted anyway. In branch B, the same Shoo! on a carrier lost the game. That is a big swing on who the Storyteller can see.
- **Branch B: v0.6 without the exploits can be harsh.**
  - At Broom 6–7 under a Searcher, each haul is a 52% coin flip, and losing it costs the box's whole route plus its only exit.
  - Better play (keeping the Sweet-3 cat free to Distract before the last haul) would help.
  - It's worth simulating the Standard win rate with Searchers who ignore noise before adopting V6-02's fix.
- **What an 8-year-old would find confusing:**
  1. Mrs Okafor "finds it gone" while three cats are holding it in front of her (V6-05).
  2. Why letting your own cat get chased out was the clever move (V6-01). It will feel like cheating to some kids and like genius to others.
  3. Paying for a Push with a Broom step, and what happens if that step fills the meter (V6-17).
  4. The three "Finding it" checks at the end of the house's turn, in order.
  5. "You must deal with the dog before doing anything else", but you can just stand there (V6-03).
  6. The tempted cat goes first, but the box needs all three carriers at once (V6-07).
