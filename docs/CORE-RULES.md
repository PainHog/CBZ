# Cat Burglars — core rules (one page)

*Version 0.3 — simulated (sim/BALANCE.md, package P6). Decisions: docs/DESIGN.md.*

**The game.** You're a gang of street cats. You just watched a human carry something into a house: something **delicious**, something **fun**, or **a box**. Get it — eaten or outside — and get every cat out again, before the **Prize Clock** runs out and without being caught.

**Your cat.** Approaches rated **3, 2, 1, 1** (any order; max 4): **Sneaky** (unnoticed: hide, slip past, snatch under a nose), **Sweet** (charm: distract, persuade, befriend), **Savage** (force, speed, fights, facing down animals, wriggling free), **Streetwise** (doors, latches, cupboards, figuring things out). Two **instincts** (d8: Curious, Knocker, Box Sitter, Hunter, Lap Cat, Foodie, Climber, Scrapper). Body: **Lean** (fits Tight gaps) or **Chonky** (carries Medium alone; +1 die to wriggle free). **Cattitude** 2 (max 5). **9 Lives**.

**Rolling.** Say what you do and how. Dice = approach **+1** if an instinct suits it (**Knack**, max +1) **+1 per helper** (max +2) **+1 for 1 Cattitude** spent, **max 5**. Then remove dice for Difficulty (**Tricky −1, Hard −2**), for a resident in your way (its rating), and for a watching human's **Attention** on **Sneaky or Sweet** rolls. At 0 or fewer, roll 2 and keep the **lower**, no better than a Scrape. Read the **highest die**: **1–3 Flop** (it doesn't work; noise) · **4–5 Scrape** (it works; noise) · **6 Clean** (it works) · **two 6s Purrfect** (it works, no Mood step, +1 Cattitude). **Push:** once per roll, reroll every die that isn't a 6; pay 1 Cattitude or 1 extra Mood step.

**Seen.** You're seen if you share a room with an awake human who isn't Distracted or holding a cat, and you're not in a hiding spot. Walking, sitting and washing in view are free. **Anything else in view is a roll**: Sneaky to do it unnoticed, Savage to just do it, Sweet if it's charm.

**The Mood: two meters, Broom and Cuddle**, each filling to the house's **Patience** (Easy/Standard 4–5, Hard 3). A roll a human sees adds steps: **Clean or Scrape 1** (a **Sneaky Clean 0**) on its approach's meter — **Sweet fills Cuddle; the rest fill Broom**; a kid watching doubles Cuddle steps. **A Flop adds 2 to the fuller meter** (its own if level): a flop makes their mood worse. One roll, one change. Also **+1 Broom** when a human answers noise you made on purpose and finds the mess, and when a human walks in on the prize disturbed.

**Shoo! (Broom full):** the human chases that cat out through the nearest entrance and **shuts it**. **Scooped! (Cuddle full):** the human picks that cat up (**Held**). Either way the cat drops what it carries (the human puts it back), that meter empties, and **Patience drops 1** (min 2). Nobody in sight? The meter stays full for the next cat seen.

**Caught.** A Held cat can't move, and its holder sees nobody else. It may wriggle free (Savage, Tricky). End of each round the holder rolls d6: **1–3** cuddles on, **4–5** shuts it in the **Den**, **6** puts it outside. A cat in the Den gets out if a friend opens the door (Streetwise, Tricky) or by itself (Streetwise, Hard). Outside cats may come back in through any open entrance. **Still inside at the end? Adopted.** (Campaigns: spend a Life and it escapes its new home before the next robbery.)

**A round.** *Start:* the Storyteller may **tempt** one cat whose room holds one of its instincts' temptations: **give in** (the instinct thing is its Action; +1 Cattitude) or **resist** (1 Cattitude; with none, give in). *Cats,* any order: **one Move** (next room, open way) and **one Action** (a roll, or a simple thing when unseen: pick up, drop, eat a portion, help, hide). **Distract** (Sweet): that human sees nobody until the round ends; each repeat on the same human is 1 die harder. **Noise** draws the closest awake human within 2 rooms at the end of the round. *House:* noise, then each human's and pet's **d6 routine**, holders' rolls, the **Prize Clock**.

**Getting in and out.** Each house has 3+ entrances (approach and Difficulty, room, size). Cats may split up. The **front door** lets in **one** cat, in round 1, behind the human. **Meow at a door** (Sweet) and a human there lets you out (not with your mouth full). Ways are **Tight** (Lean cats, Small things), **Normal** (up to Medium) or **Big**.

**The prize.** It starts in the human's arms; the Prize Clock says where it's put down, when it moves and when it's gone. A spot can take several steps (up on the counter, then the foil), one working roll each. **Small** (one cat, in its mouth), **Medium** (a Chonky cat or two cats; no climbing), **Large** (three cats, or two with a Chonky; Big ways). **Moving it past a human is a roll** (one leader, helpers add dice). Food has **portions** (set per cat in the gang); eating one is an Action.

**Residents.** Humans: Attention 0–2, a d6 routine, sometimes a trait (dozes, busy, kid). Pets: rating 1–3 and a d6 routine. **Dog:** barks (noise) and charges; a cat starting its turn with it must leave or deal with it (Savage drives it off for 3 rounds, Sweet befriends a soft one, Sneaky slips past for this turn); dogs smell hidden cats. **House cat:** guards a room the same way. **Parrot:** squawks while it sees a cat. **Robot vacuum:** wanders.

**Ending.** Lights Out comes 2 rounds after the Prize Clock. The robbery ends when no free cat is inside, or at Lights Out. **Clean Getaway:** prize got, nobody left behind. **Messy:** prize got but someone was left behind, or at least half the food eaten. **Busted:** less. **Gang size:** with 3 cats, Patience +1 and Clock +1; with 5, Clock −1.
