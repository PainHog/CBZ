# Cat Burglars — core rules (summary)

*Version 0.7 — simulated (sim/BALANCE.md, package P9) and playtested six times (sim/playtests/). Decisions: docs/DESIGN.md. Rulings: book/REVIEW.md. The rulebook chapters are the full rules; this is the summary.*

**The game.** You're a gang of street cats. You just watched a human carry something into a house: something **delicious**, something **fun**, or **a box**. Get it — eaten or outside — and get every cat out again, before the **Prize Clock** runs out and without being caught.

**Your cat** (random tables: rulebook Chapter 2). Approaches rated **3, 2, 1, 1** (max 4): **Sneaky** (unnoticed: hide, slip past, snatch), **Sweet** (charm: distract, persuade, befriend), **Savage** (force, speed, facing down animals, wriggling free), **Streetwise** (doors, latches, bags, figuring things out). Two different **instincts**. Body: **Lean** (fits Tight gaps) or **Chonky** (carries Medium alone; +1 die to wriggle free). **Cattitude** 2 at the start of every robbery (max 5; what's left is lost). **9 Lives**.

**Rolling.** Dice = approach **+1** Knack (an instinct suits it; max +1) **+1 per helper** (max +2; helping is the helper's Action, in the same room, and never a roll of its own) **+1** for 1 Cattitude, **max 5**. Then remove dice: Difficulty (**Tricky −1, Hard −2**), a pet you're dealing with (its rating), and on **Sneaky or Sweet** rolls the **highest** Attention among the humans watching. At 0 or fewer, roll 2 and keep the **lower**, no better than a Scrape. Read the **highest die**: **1–3 Flop** (it doesn't work; noise) · **4–5 Scrape** (it works; noise) · **6 Clean** (it works) · **two 6s Purrfect** (it works, no Mood step, +1 Cattitude). **Push:** once per roll, reroll every die that isn't a 6; the roller pays 1 Cattitude, or 1 step on that roll's meter (seen or not), which goes on with the roll's own steps after the reroll (a Flop's fuller meter is read before the Push).

**Seen.** You're seen if you share a room with an awake human who isn't Distracted or holding a cat, and you're not in a hiding spot (every room has them unless the house says not; moving or doing anything else ends hiding; a cat holding the prize can't hide). Walking, sitting, washing, and going in or out through an entrance that needs no roll are free in view. **Any other Action in view is a roll**: Sneaky to do it unnoticed, Savage to just do it, Sweet if it's charm.

**The Mood: two meters, Broom and Cuddle**, each filling to the house's **Patience**. A roll a human sees adds steps: **Clean or Scrape 1** (a **Sneaky Clean 0**) — **Sweet fills Cuddle; the rest fill Broom**; a kid who sees the roll — even one aimed at that kid — doubles its Cuddle steps (once); a Distracted human sees nothing. **A Flop a human sees adds 2 to the fuller meter** (its own if level). One roll, one change. A roll that fills a meter still does what it did, but the trigger happens before the cat moves on.

**Shoo! (Broom full):** the watching human (highest Attention; ties, Storyteller's pick) chases that cat out through the nearest entrance (ties likewise) and **shuts it**. A meter filled with no roll (spotted, a find, a mess) falls on a cat that human can see, the Storyteller's pick. **Scooped! (Cuddle full):** they pick that cat up (**Held**). Either way the cat drops what it carries (the human puts it back where the Clock last put it), that meter empties, and **Patience drops 1** (min 2). Nobody in sight? The meter stays full for the next cat seen.

**Caught.** A Held cat can't move. Its holder stays put — no routine, noise, Searching or finding — but still sees their room. It may wriggle free (Savage, Tricky). End of each round the holder rolls d6: **1–3** cuddles on, **4–5** shuts it in the **Den** (the holder stays in that room this round), **6** puts it out through the nearest entrance (left open). The Den's door is shut while a cat is in it: a friend in the next room opens it (Streetwise, Tricky); the cat alone needs Streetwise, Hard. Outside cats may come back in through any open entrance. **Any cat still inside at the end — held, shut in or free — is adopted.** (Campaign: spend a Life to escape the new home before the next robbery.)

**A round.**
- *Start:* the **Prize Clock** acts: the human puts the prize in its place and stays in that room this round (unless they answer a noise). If the prize isn't where the Clock last put it (a prize in the cats' paws never is), the human goes there instead, stays, and finds it gone at once (+1 Broom, Searching). Then the Storyteller may **tempt** one cat whose room has one of its instincts' temptations, naming the cat and the temptation (never the same cat two rounds running); if it gives in, it acts first this round. **Give in** (+1 Cattitude; the instinct thing is its Action, done before it moves, there; a carrier drops the prize in that room first): *Curious* rummages (noise) · *Knocker* knocks it off (noise and a mess) · *Box Sitter* gets in (quiet; hidden) · *Hunter* chases it (noise) · *Lap Cat* goes for a fuss (its player picks a human there: Distracted this round, +1 Cuddle, 2 for a kid; nobody there: a sunny spot) · *Foodie* eats it (quiet) · *Climber* goes up high (quiet; hidden) · *Scrapper* squares up (noise; that isn't dealing with a dog). Curious, Knocker, Foodie and Scrapper in view add +1 Broom; giving in is never a roll. Every room with an awake dog or house cat tempts Scrappers. Or **resist** (1 Cattitude; with none, give in).
- *Cats,* any order, each one **Move** (next room, open way) and one **Action** (a roll, or when unseen a simple thing: pick up, drop, hide, make a noise), in either order. **Distract** (Sweet) a human in your room: they see nobody until the House phase; each earlier distraction of them that worked this robbery takes a die. **Make a noise** on purpose (knock something over) and you leave a **mess**.
- *House:* 1) **Pets:** a sleeping dog wakes if there was noise in its room or next door this round; an awake dog with a cat in its room barks (noise) and stays; otherwise it rolls its routine. 2) **Noise**, in the order it was made: each draws the closest awake human within 2 rooms who isn't already there or holding a cat (one noise each; ties, Storyteller), and they go straight there and stay. 3) Every other human rolls their **d6 routine** (straight there; they see only that room) or Searches; holders roll. Humans and pets pass closed doors and leave them as they were. 4) **Finding it** (below).

**Finding it.** At the end of the House phase: if a **Searching** human (not holding a cat) can see a cat, **+1 Broom** (once a round). If the prize is **disturbed** (a step done, a portion gone, or not where the Clock last put it), every awake human not yet Searching or holding a cat who is in its room, or in the room where the Clock put it, finds it: **+1 Broom** (once, however many) and they start **Searching**. A human in a room with a **mess** finds it: +1 Broom and the mess is cleared.

**Searching** (for the rest of the robbery), instead of a routine: while the prize is inside, stay with it if it's in their room, else step into its room if it's next door; else stay with a cat they can see, else step into a next room with an unhidden cat (a cat shut in the Den doesn't count); else roll the routine.

**In and out.** Each house has 3+ entrances. Cats start outside. Going through an entrance, in or out, is your **Move**; if it lists an approach, that roll is also your **Action**; going out through one that lists none can be your Move or your Action. Cats may split up. A **noisy** entrance makes a noise each time a cat goes through. A shut entrance can be opened again from inside (Streetwise, Hard). The **front door** works in the rounds the house lists (usually round 1) and lets in **one** cat: Sneaky past the human, or Sweet (they let you in), in view of that human. **Meow at a door** (Sweet) and a human there lets you out — not with your mouth full: a cat carrying something can't be Sweet. Ways are **Tight** (Lean cats, Small things), **Normal** (up to Medium) or **Big**; a **climb** entrance (a window) takes Small things only. Noise carries through closed doors.

**The prize.** It starts in the human's arms. A spot can take several steps (up on the counter, then the foil): each needs one working roll, and **a step done stays done for the whole gang**. Getting it down isn't climbing. **Small** (one cat), **Medium** (a Chonky cat or two; no climbing while carrying), **Large** (three cats, or two with a Chonky; Big ways). Carriers each pick it up as their Action, then hold it without spending Actions until they let go, give in, are Shooed or are Scooped; they move together. **Hauling:** moving a **Medium or Large** prize into another room or out through an entrance is a roll every time, seen or not — a **Small** one only when a human in either room sees it. It is every carrier's Action that round: one carrier rolls **Sneaky** (quietly) or **Savage** (heave), plain, the others help (pushing is a Knocker's Knack). If the entrance lists a roll, that's the roll. It works: they all move, and its noise is in the room it ends up in. Fail: it doesn't move. Hauling out of an awake dog's room counts as leaving; a failed haul there is chased back and drops the prize. If the leader is Shooed or Scooped, the others let go and the prize goes back where the Clock put it. Food has **portions** (set per cat); eating one is an Action and **always a roll** (Sneaky quietly, Savage wolfing it; Foodie's Knack).

**Residents.** Humans: Attention 0–2, a d6 routine, maybe a trait (dozes, busy, kid). **Dog** (rating 1–3, d6 routine of rooms, *stay* and *sniff* = one room toward the nearest cat, ties Storyteller's pick; dogs smell hidden cats): a cat that enters its room or starts its turn there must deal with it before anything else (helping counts; with no Action left, first thing next turn), and a cat that ends its turn there without dealing with it is chased back the way it came; the roll removes its rating. **Savage** drives it off: it hides for 3 rounds counting this one, then is back where it started, awake. **Sweet** befriends a soft one, for the whole gang, all robbery. **Sneaky** slips past, this turn. A failed roll: it chases you back the way you came (no Move needed; a carrier drops the prize). **House cat:** guards its room the same way. **Parrot** (never moves): squawks (noise) at the end of a round it saw an unhidden cat; cover its cage (Streetwise, Tricky) to silence it. **Robot vacuum:** wanders a room a round on its list (Pets step); tempts Hunters; a cat can hop on (simple Action) and ride it, hidden.

**Ending.** The prize is gone at the start of the Clock's last round — unless the cats are holding it, and then it stays theirs to get out. **Lights Out** falls at the end of the second round after that. The robbery ends when no free cat is inside, or at Lights Out. Food is **got** when it's outside or every portion is eaten. **Clean Getaway:** prize got, nobody left behind. **Messy:** prize got but someone left behind, or at least half the food eaten. **Busted:** less. **Gang size:** 3 cats — Patience +1, the prize goes 2 rounds later, and each cat starts with 3 Cattitude; 5 cats — Patience −1 and the prize goes 1 round sooner.
