# Cat Burglars — core rules (one page)

*Version 0.5 — simulated (sim/BALANCE.md, package P7) and playtested twice (sim/playtests/). Decisions: docs/DESIGN.md. Rulings: book/REVIEW.md.*

**The game.** You're a gang of street cats. You just watched a human carry something into a house: something **delicious**, something **fun**, or **a box**. Get it — eaten or outside — and get every cat out again, before the **Prize Clock** runs out and without being caught.

**Your cat** (random tables: rulebook Chapter 2). Approaches rated **3, 2, 1, 1** (max 4): **Sneaky** (unnoticed: hide, slip past, snatch), **Sweet** (charm: distract, persuade, befriend), **Savage** (force, speed, facing down animals, wriggling free), **Streetwise** (doors, latches, bags, figuring things out). Two different **instincts**. Body: **Lean** (fits Tight gaps) or **Chonky** (carries Medium alone; +1 die to wriggle free). **Cattitude** 2 at the start of every robbery (max 5; what's left is lost). **9 Lives**.

**Rolling.** Dice = approach **+1** Knack (an instinct suits it; max +1) **+1 per helper** (max +2; a helper is in the same room and doesn't roll, seen or not) **+1** for 1 Cattitude, **max 5**. Then remove dice: Difficulty (**Tricky −1, Hard −2**), a pet in your way (its rating), and on **Sneaky or Sweet** rolls the **highest** Attention among the humans watching. At 0 or fewer, roll 2 and keep the **lower**, no better than a Scrape. Read the **highest die**: **1–3 Flop** (it doesn't work; noise) · **4–5 Scrape** (it works; noise) · **6 Clean** (it works) · **two 6s Purrfect** (it works, no Mood step, +1 Cattitude). **Push:** once per roll, reroll every die that isn't a 6; pay 1 Cattitude, or 1 step on that roll's meter (seen or not).

**Seen.** You're seen if you share a room with an awake human who isn't Distracted or holding a cat, and you're not in a hiding spot (every room has them unless the house says not; moving or doing anything else ends hiding). Walking, sitting and washing in view are free. **Any other Action in view is a roll**: Sneaky to do it unnoticed, Savage to just do it, Sweet if it's charm.

**The Mood: two meters, Broom and Cuddle**, each filling to the house's **Patience**. A roll a human sees adds steps: **Clean or Scrape 1** (a **Sneaky Clean 0**) — **Sweet fills Cuddle; the rest fill Broom**; a kid who sees the roll — even one aimed at that kid — doubles its Cuddle steps (once); a Distracted human sees nothing. **A Flop adds 2 to the fuller meter** (its own if level). One roll, one change. Also **+1 Broom** each time a human answers a noise made on purpose and finds the mess, and each time a human walks into the prize's room and finds it disturbed (a step done, a portion gone, or the prize not where the Clock last put it). **A human who finds it disturbed starts Searching** (see Residents).

**Shoo! (Broom full):** the watching human (highest Attention; ties, Storyteller's pick) chases that cat out through the nearest entrance (ties likewise) and **shuts it**. **Scooped! (Cuddle full):** they pick that cat up (**Held**). Either way the cat drops what it carries (the human puts it back), that meter empties, and **Patience drops 1** (min 2). Nobody in sight? The meter stays full for the next cat seen.

**Caught.** A Held cat can't move, and its holder sees nobody else and skips their routine. It may wriggle free (Savage, Tricky). End of each round the holder rolls d6: **1–3** cuddles on, **4–5** shuts it in the **Den**, **6** puts it outside. The Den's door is shut while a cat is in it: a friend in the next room opens it (Streetwise, Tricky); the cat alone needs Streetwise, Hard. Outside cats may come back in through any open entrance. **Any cat still inside at the end — held, shut in or free — is adopted.** (Campaign: spend a Life to escape the new home before the next robbery.)

**A round.** *Start:* the Prize Clock acts: the human puts the prize in its place and stays in that room this round. If the cats already have it, the human goes to its place instead, finds it gone (+1 Broom), and starts Searching. The Storyteller may **tempt** one cat whose room has one of its instincts' temptations. **Give in** (+1 Cattitude; the instinct thing is your Action, done first, there): *Curious* rummages (noise) · *Knocker* knocks it off (noise and a mess) · *Box Sitter* gets in (quiet; hidden) · *Hunter* chases it (noise) · *Lap Cat* goes for a fuss (a human there is Distracted this round, +1 Cuddle; nobody there: a sunny spot) · *Foodie* eats it (quiet) · *Climber* goes up high (quiet; hidden) · *Scrapper* squares up (noise; a dog there turns angry). Curious, Knocker, Foodie and Scrapper in view add +1 Broom; giving in is never a roll. Or **resist** (1 Cattitude; with none, give in). *Cats,* any order, each one **Move** (next room, open way) and one **Action** (a roll, or when unseen a simple thing: pick up, drop, eat a portion, hide, make a noise), in either order. Carriers each pick the prize up as their Action and move together as their Moves. **Distract** (Sweet): that human sees nobody until the round ends; each earlier distraction of them this robbery takes a die. *House:* **noise** in the order it was made — each draws the closest awake human within 2 rooms who isn't already there (one noise each; ties, Storyteller) — A human who answered a noise stays there. Then each other human's and pet's **d6 routine** (straight there this round; they see only that room), holders' rolls.

**In and out.** Each house has 3+ entrances. Cats start outside. Going through an entrance, in or out, is your **Move**; if it lists an approach, that roll is also your **Action**. Cats may split up. A shut entrance can be opened again from inside (Streetwise, Hard). The **front door** works in the rounds the house lists (usually round 1) and lets in **one** cat: Sneaky past the human, or Sweet (they let you in), in view of that human. **Meow at a door** (Sweet) and a human there lets you out — not with your mouth full: a cat carrying something can't be Sweet. Ways are **Tight** (Lean cats, Small things), **Normal** (up to Medium) or **Big**.

**The prize.** It starts in the human's arms. A spot can take several steps (up on the counter, then the foil): each needs one working roll, and **a step done stays done for the whole gang**. Getting it down isn't climbing. **Small** (one cat), **Medium** (a Chonky cat or two; no climbing while carrying), **Large** (three cats, or two with a Chonky; Big ways). **Moving it past a human is a roll** (one leader, helpers add dice). Food has **portions** (set per cat); eating one is an Action.

**Residents.** Humans: Attention 0–2, a d6 routine, maybe a trait (dozes, busy, kid). **Searching** humans skip their routine: they stay in a room with a cat in sight, or step into a next room with one (the carriers first), or else follow their routine. **Dog** (rating 1–3, d6 routine of rooms, *stay* and *sniff* = one room toward the nearest cat): a sleeping dog does nothing until there's noise in its room or next door. An awake dog barks (noise) at the end of any round a cat is in its room, and a cat that enters or starts its turn there must leave or deal with it before anything else (Savage drives it off for 3 rounds, Sweet befriends a soft one, Sneaky slips past this turn); dogs smell hidden cats. **House cat:** guards its room the same way. **Parrot** (never moves): squawks (noise) at the end of a round it saw an unhidden cat; cover its cage (Streetwise, Tricky) to silence it. **Robot vacuum:** wanders.

**Ending.** The prize is gone at the start of the Clock's last round; **Lights Out** falls at the end of the second round after that. The robbery ends when no free cat is inside, or at Lights Out. **Clean Getaway:** prize got, nobody left behind. **Messy:** prize got but someone left behind, or at least half the food eaten. **Busted:** less. **Gang size:** 3 cats — Patience +1 and the prize goes 1 round later; 5 cats — it goes 1 round sooner.
