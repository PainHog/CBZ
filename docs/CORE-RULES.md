# Nine Lives Gang — core rules (summary)

*Version 0.9 — simulated (sim/BALANCE.md, package P13) and playtested (sim/playtests/). Decisions: docs/DESIGN.md. Rulings: book/REVIEW.md. The rulebook chapters are the full rules; this is the summary.*

**The game.** You're a gang of thug cats. You just watched a human carry something into a house: something **delicious**, something **fun**, something **shiny**, or **a box**. Break in, grab the loot — eat it there or get it outside — then leg it over the fences to the hideout with as much of it as you can carry, before animal control **pinches** you.

**Your cat** (random tables: rulebook Chapter 2). Approaches rated **3, 2, 1, 1** (max 4): **Sneaky** (unnoticed: hide, slip past, snatch), **Sweet** (charm: distract, persuade, befriend), **Savage** (force, speed, facing down animals, wriggling free), **Streetwise** (doors, latches, bags, figuring things out). Two different **instincts**. Body: **Lean** (fits Tight gaps) or **Chonky** (carries Medium alone; +1 die to wriggle free). **Cattitude** 2 at the start of every robbery (3 in a gang of three, plus the gang's Rep bonus; max 5; what's left is lost). **9 Lives**.

**Rolling.** Dice = approach **+1** Knack (an instinct suits it; max +1) **+1 per helper** (max +2; helping is the helper's Action, in the same room, and never a roll of its own), **max 5**. Then remove dice: Difficulty (**Tricky −1, Hard −2**), a pet you're dealing with (its rating), and on **Sneaky or Sweet** rolls the **highest** Attention among the humans watching. At 0 or fewer, roll 2 and keep the **lower**, no better than a Scrape. Read the **highest die**: **1–3 Flop** (it doesn't work; noise; that cat can't try the same thing again this round) · **4–5 Scrape** (it works; noise) · **6 Clean** (it works) · **two 6s Purrfect** (it works, no Mood step, +1 Cattitude). **Push:** once per roll, reroll every die that isn't a 6; the roller pays 1 Cattitude, or 1 step on that roll's meter (seen or not), which always lands (even on a Purrfect; a kid doesn't double it). Cattitude buys only a Push or resisting a temptation.

**Seen.** You're seen if you share a room with an awake human who isn't Distracted, and you're not in a hiding spot (a human holding a cat sees you but can't chase or pick up anyone else) (every room has them unless the house says not; moving or doing anything else ends hiding; a cat holding the prize can't hide; hiding in view is a Sneaky roll). Walking, sitting, washing, staring, and going in or out through an entrance that needs no roll are free in view. **Any other Action in view is a roll**: Sneaky to do it unnoticed, Savage to just do it, Sweet if it's charm.

**The Mood: two meters, Broom and Cuddle**, each filling to the house's **Patience**. A roll a human sees adds steps: **Clean or Scrape 1** (a **Sneaky Clean 0**) — **Sweet fills Cuddle; the rest fill Broom**; a kid who sees the roll doubles its Cuddle steps (once); a Distracted human sees nothing. **A Flop a human sees adds 2 to the fuller meter** (its own if level). One roll, one change; steps past a full meter are lost. A roll that fills a meter still does what it did, but the trigger happens before the cat moves on.

**Shoo! (Broom full):** the watching human (highest Attention; ties, the Storyteller) chases that cat out through the **nearest entrance it fits through** that works this round, opening it if it's shut, and **shuts it** — unless it's the house's last entrance still open. They stay in that entrance's room this round, deaf to noise. A meter filled with no roll (spotted, a find, a mess) falls on a cat that human can see, the Storyteller's pick. **Scooped! (Cuddle full):** they pick that cat up (**Held**); a Distract that fills Cuddle ends in the Scoop, not a Distraction. Either way: **if the cat carried the prize**, the human puts it back where the Clock last put it, every step to do again (eaten portions stay eaten); that meter empties; **Patience drops 1** (min 2); **the other meter drops to one below the new Patience** if it's at or over it. Nobody with free hands in sight? The meter stays full (steps on it are lost) until someone can see a cat, then acts at once; both full, Broom first. A held cat whose own roll fills Broom is put outside.

**Caught.** A Held cat can't move. Its holder stays put — no routine, noise, Searching or finding — but still sees their room. It may wriggle free (Savage, Tricky; friends in the room may help). In the house's turn the holder rolls d6: **1–3** cuddles on, **4–5** shuts it in the **Den** (the holder stays just outside this round), **6** puts it out through the nearest entrance that works (left open). The Den's door is shut while a cat is in it: a friend in the next room opens it (Streetwise, Tricky); the cat alone needs Streetwise, Hard. Outside cats may come back in through any open entrance, from next round.

**A round.**
- *Start:* the **Prize Clock** acts: the human puts the prize in its place and stays with it this round, answering no noise. If the prize isn't where the Clock last put it (a prize in the cats' paws never is), the human goes to where the Clock last put it instead, stays, and finds it gone at once (+1 Broom, Searching). Then the Storyteller may **tempt** one free cat inside whose room has one of its instincts' temptations (every room has shiny things for Curious and Knocker cats; a cat that must deal with a pet can't be tempted), naming the cat and the temptation (never the same cat two rounds running); if it gives in, it acts first this round. **Give in** (+1 Cattitude; the instinct thing is its Action, done before it moves, there; a carrier drops the prize in that room first; a give-in's Mood step never fills a meter — it stops one short): *Curious* rummages (noise) · *Knocker* knocks it off (noise; a mess if nobody saw) · *Box Sitter* gets in (quiet; hidden) · *Hunter* chases it (noise) · *Lap Cat* goes for a fuss (its player picks a human there: Distracted until the house's turn, and it counts as a distraction of them; +1 Cuddle, 2 for a kid; nobody there: a sunny spot) · *Foodie* eats it (quiet) · *Climber* goes up high (quiet; hidden) · *Scrapper* squares up (noise; that isn't dealing with a dog). Curious, Knocker, Foodie and Scrapper in view add +1 Broom; giving in is never a roll. Every room with an awake (unbefriended) dog or house cat tempts Scrappers. Or **resist** (1 Cattitude; with none, give in).
- *Cats,* any order, each one **Move** (next room, open way) and one **Action** (a roll, or when unseen a simple thing: pick up, drop, hide, make a noise), in either order; held and shut-in cats can only try to get free. **Distract** (Sweet) a human in your room: they see nobody until the house's turn; each earlier distraction of them that worked this robbery takes a die. **Make a noise** on purpose (knock something over; it's a noise whatever you roll) and you leave a **mess** (one per room; found once).
- *House:* 1) **Pets:** a sleeping dog wakes if there was noise in its room or next door this round; an awake, unbefriended dog with a cat in its room barks (noise) and stays; otherwise it rolls its routine; a parrot that saw an unhidden cat this round squawks (noise). 2) **Noise**, in the order it was made: each draws the closest awake human within 2 rooms who isn't already there, holding a cat or staying put this round (several noises in one room count once; one noise each; ties, the higher Attention, then a die), and they go straight there and stay. 3) Every other human rolls their **d6 routine** (straight there; they see only that room) or Searches; dozers roll to doze every round; holders roll. Humans and pets pass closed doors and leave them as they were. 4) **Finding it** (below).

**Finding it.** At the end of the house's turn: if a **Searching** human (not holding a cat) can see a cat (a held cat counts), **+1 Broom** (once a round). If the prize is **disturbed** (a step done, a portion gone, or not where the Clock last put it), every awake human not yet Searching or holding a cat who is in its room, or in the room where the Clock put it, finds it: **+1 Broom** (once, however many) and they start **Searching**. A human in a room with a **mess** finds it: +1 Broom and the mess is cleared.

**Searching** (for the rest of the robbery), instead of a routine: while the prize is inside (a prize that's all eaten isn't), stay with it if it's in their room, else step into its room if it's next door (they still answer noise first); else stay with a cat they can see, else step into a next room with an unhidden cat (a cat shut in the Den doesn't count); else roll the routine.

**In and out.** Each house has 3+ entrances. Cats start outside. Going through an entrance, in or out, is your **Move**; if it lists an approach, that roll is also your **Action**; going out through one that lists none can be your Move or your Action, but you can't go out and back in on one turn. An entrance's roll is seen from the room it leads into; a roll to open an inner door is seen from the cat's room. Cats may split up. A **noisy** entrance makes a noise each time a cat goes through. A shut entrance can be opened again from inside (Streetwise, Hard). The **front door** works in the rounds the house lists (usually round 1) and lets in **one** cat a round: Sneaky (Hard) past the human, or Sweet (plain), in view of that human; a failed try uses up that round. **Meow at a door** (Sweet) where a human is — any door, not a window or skylight — and they let you out; not with your mouth full: a cat carrying something can't be Sweet. Ways are **Tight** (Lean cats, Small things), **Normal** (up to Medium) or **Big**; a **climb** entrance (a window) takes Small things only. Noise carries through closed doors.

**The prize.** It starts in the human's arms, out of reach. A spot can take several steps (up on the counter, then the foil): each needs one working roll, and **a step done stays done for the whole gang**. **Small** (one cat), **Medium** (a Chonky cat or two; no climbing while carrying), **Large** (three cats, or two with a Chonky; Big ways). Carriers each pick it up as their Action, then hold it without spending Actions until they let go, give in, are Shooed or are Scooped; they move together. **Hauling:** moving a **Medium or Large** prize into another room or out through an entrance is a roll every time, seen or not — a **Small** one only when a human in either room sees it. It is every carrier's Action that round: one carrier rolls **Sneaky** or **Savage**, plain unless the house says (the cake wobbles: Tricky); the other carriers help, and so may any other cat in the room (+2 at most); only carriers move with it; humans in either room see it (pushing is a Knocker's Knack). If the entrance lists a roll, that's the roll. It works: the carriers move, and its noise is in the room it ends up in. Fail: it doesn't move. Hauling out of an awake dog's room counts as leaving (the dog takes no dice off it); a failed haul there is chased back and drops the prize where they end up; hauling into one leaves the carriers to deal with the dog next turn. If the leader is Shooed or Scooped, the others let go and the prize goes back where the Clock put it. Nobody can pick up or eat it until every step is done. Food has **portions** (set per cat); eating one is an Action and **always a roll** (Sneaky quietly, Savage wolfing it; plain unless the house says; a Foodie's Knack either way).

**The house ends.** The prize is gone at the start of the Clock's last round — unless the cats are holding it, and then it stays theirs to get out. **Lights Out** falls at the end of the second round after that. The house part ends at Lights Out, or at the end of a round when no free cat is inside and the prize is out, eaten or gone, or the gang runs for it. **Any cat still inside then — held, shut in or free — is pinched** (animal control collects it).

**The getaway** (Chapter 9). The **loot**: food in its uneaten portions (if it was got out), else Small 1, Medium 2, Large 3 pieces; each cat carries one piece, a Chonky cat two; the rest is left. The house's **route** has four obstacles to the hideout. Each getaway round: 1) **Cats** go over the next obstacle (its roll; groups of up to three, one leads and the rest help and go with it), help, or pick up a dropped piece. A cat carrying loot loses 1 die (a Chonky cat with one piece doesn't); it may drop a piece first to lose the die. A Flop: stuck, and each carrier drops a piece there. No meters out here; a Push costs Cattitude. 2) **The warden** arrives at the house at the end of round 1 (round 2 if nobody was Shooed or Scooped tonight), then moves 1 space a round, 2 if any cat Flopped. 3) **Cornered:** a cat on or behind the warden's space dodges (Sneaky, Savage or Sweet, plain) to the space ahead of the warden, or on a Flop is **pinched**, loot and all; loot lying there is gone. It ends when everyone is home or pinched.

**Cred** (Chapter 9) — showing off, scored per cat on the tracker; it never changes how the night went. **Tag a room** (mark your territory): 1 Cred, one tag a room for the gang; it's your Action: unseen, a simple thing; in view, a roll (Sneaky to do it unnoticed, Savage to just do it) and +1 Cred if it works. A tag is a mess. **The rivals' tag:** each house names a rival gang and the room they've tagged; tagging over it scores 1 more. **The catnip stash:** each house says where. It tempts every cat in its room, whatever its instincts (giving in: a noise, +1 Broom in view, like Curious), or a cat takes it on purpose as its Action (in view, a roll); getting it is a noise and scores 1. It gets **the zoomies** until the house part ends: +1 die on every roll (max 5), every roll it makes is a noise, it can't resist a temptation, and its Cred counts double. One stash, one cat. **On the getaway** the rivals hang about on one obstacle (the house says which): loot dropped there is nicked at once; the cat who leads a group over it with a Clean or Purrfect scores 1. A Purrfect on any obstacle scores 1. **The Cat’s Whiskers:** the most Cred at the end of the night (ties share it).

**Ending.** Count the loot home plus the portions eaten in the house. **Clean Getaway:** all of it, nobody pinched. **Messy:** at least half, or all of it but someone pinched. **Busted:** less. A pinched cat goes to the **pound** (campaign: spend a Life to bust out before the next robbery). **Rep** (campaign): +1 for a Clean Getaway, +1 for a night with 5 or more Cred that wasn't Busted; at 3, 6 and 9 Rep each cat starts with +1 Cattitude. **Gang size:** 3 cats — Patience +1, the prize goes 2 rounds later, and each cat starts with 3 Cattitude; 5 cats — Patience −1; each ready-made house prints its own numbers.
