# Cat Burglars — core rules (one page)

*Version 0.1 — starting numbers, to be tuned by the simulator. Decisions: `docs/DESIGN.md`.*

**The game.** You're a gang of street cats. You just watched a human carry something into a house: something **delicious**, something **fun**, or **a box**. Get it — eaten or outside — and get every cat out again, before the **Prize Clock** runs out and without being caught.

**Your cat.** Four approaches rated **3, 2, 1, 1** (any order; max 4): **Sneaky** (unseen: hide, slip past, snatch under a nose), **Sweet** (charm: distract, persuade, calm, befriend), **Savage** (force, speed, fights, facing down animals, wriggling free), **Streetwise** (doors, latches, cupboards, figuring things out). Two **instincts** (d8: Curious, Knocker, Box Sitter, Hunter, Lap Cat, Foodie, Climber, Scrapper). A body: **Lean** (fits Tight gaps) or **Chonky** (carries Medium alone; +1 die to wriggle free). **Cattitude** 2 (max 5). **9 Lives**.

**Rolling.** Say what you do and how (the approach). Roll that many d6, **+1** if one of your instincts suits it (**Knack**, max +1), **+1 per helper** (max +2), **max 5 dice**. Difficulty removes dice: **Tricky −1, Hard −2**; a resident in your way removes dice equal to its rating; a human watching removes their **Attention** from Sneaky rolls. At 0 dice roll 1, and a 6 counts as a Scrape. Read the **highest die**:

| Highest die | Result | |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | **Flop** | It doesn't work. Noise. |
| 4–5 | **Scrape** | It works. Noise. |
| 6 | **Clean** | It works. |
| two 6s | **Purrfect** | It works, no Mood step, +1 Cattitude. |

**Push (at all costs).** Once per roll, after rolling: reroll every die that isn't a 6. Pay **1 Cattitude**, or pay with **1 extra Mood step** in that roll's direction.

**The Mood.** One track for the household: **Broom ← … ← Calm (0) → … → Cuddle**, each end at the house's **Patience** (Easy 5, Standard 4, Hard 3). **Being seen isn't being caught; being seen *doing something* is.** You are **seen** if you're in the same room as an awake human who isn't Distracted or holding a cat, and you're not in a hiding spot. Walking, sitting and washing in view are free. If a human sees you roll:
- **Clean or Scrape:** 1 step toward your approach's lean — **Sweet leans Cuddle; Sneaky, Savage and Streetwise lean Broom.** A **Sneaky Clean** takes no step (they never noticed).
- **Flop:** 2 steps toward whichever end the Mood is already nearer (your lean at Calm). *A flop makes their mood worse.*
- Each roll moves the Mood once, however many humans saw it.

**Shoo! (Broom end):** the human chases the cat that caused it out through the nearest entrance and **shuts that entrance**. **Scooped! (Cuddle end):** the human picks that cat up — it's **Held**. Either way the cat drops what it carries, the Mood returns to Calm, and **Patience drops by 1** (min 2).

**Caught.** A **Held** cat can't move, and its holder sees nobody else. On its turn it may wriggle free (Savage, Tricky). At the end of each round the holder rolls d6: **1–3** keeps cuddling, **4–5** shuts it in the **Den** (**Shut In**), **6** puts it outside. A Shut In cat gets out if another cat opens the door (Streetwise, Tricky) or by itself (Streetwise, Hard). A cat outside may come back in through any open entrance. **Lives:** once per robbery, when you'd be Held, Shooed or Shut In, spend a Life: you slip free, hidden nearby, dropping what you carry. **Still Held or Shut In at the end? You're adopted.**

**A round.** *Start:* the Storyteller may **tempt** one cat in a room with a temptation matching one of its instincts. **Give in** (your Action is the instinct thing; +1 Cattitude) or **resist** (spend 1 Cattitude; with none, give in). *Cats:* every free cat inside, in any order, gets **one Move** (to the next room through an open way) and **one Action** (one roll, or one simple thing: pick up, drop, eat a portion, help, or hide when nobody sees). *House:* each human answers the closest **noise** within two rooms by walking into that room, or rolls their d6 **routine**; each pet rolls its routine; holders roll; the **Prize Clock** ticks.

**Getting in.** Every house has 3+ entrances (approach and Difficulty, the room it leads to, its size). Cats may split up. The front door works only in round 1 (slip in behind the human). **Ways** are **Tight** (Lean cats, Small things), **Normal** (up to Medium), or **Big** (anything).

**Together.** One cat may **help** another's roll (+1 die; it shares the consequences). Several cats doing the same thing together: one leader rolls with +1 die per helper (max +2), the result applies to all, the Mood moves once.

**The prize.** **Small** (one cat, in its mouth), **Medium** (a Chonky cat or two cats; no climbing while carrying), **Large** (three cats, or two with a Chonky; Big ways only). Food has **portions**; eating one is an Action. A cat with its mouth full can't be Sweet. The Prize Clock says when the human moves it and when it's gone.

**Residents.** Humans: **Attention** 0–2, a d6 routine, a Den. Pets: **rating** 1–3, a d6 routine and a way to beat them. **Dog:** barks (noise) and charges — a cat starting its turn with an angry dog must use its Move to leave or its Action to deal with it (Savage drives it off, Sweet befriends a soft one, Sneaky slips past); dogs sniff out hidden cats. **House cat:** guards a room. **Parrot:** squawks (noise) while it sees a cat. **Robot vacuum:** wanders, can be ridden. Driven-off pets hide for 3 rounds.

**Ending.** The robbery ends when no free cat is inside. **Clean Getaway:** prize got, nobody left behind. **Messy Getaway:** prize got (or part of it), but someone was left behind or some was lost. **Busted:** no prize.
